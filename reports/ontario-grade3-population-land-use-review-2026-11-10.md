# Grade 3 population and land-use task review

The [machine-readable dossier](ontario-grade3-population-land-use-review-2026-11-10.json) accounts for B1.3 and B3.6 through the existing map and land-purpose claims at Ontario ages 8–9. Four component pairs extend existing contexts; no new topic, interval, dependency or teaching guide is needed.

The two regional cases are Nipissing First Nation and Niagara Falls. The Nation’s July 2022 table describes **members living on reserve by named community**, a defined population subset, and separately reports homes. Niagara’s 2021 map reports resident counts per census dissemination area. The task keeps those populations, dates and unequal map areas explicit: neither colour nor count establishes density, household size or all residents in the Nation’s communities. Selected population groups are located on actual maps; they are not a claim to an exhaustive regional census.

Actual Nation trail/road/work accounts and Niagara’s existing park/trail inventory support selected land-use comparisons. Unknown land remains unclassified. The prepared packet must include the actual map keys, year, north reference and location annotations; the raw exports alone are insufficient. Comparing visual size across different scales, inventing residential uses from blank areas, and treating every park as City-owned are excluded.

The source-fit task includes broad globe orientation, interpretation and a labelled thematic comparison with a key. Its evidence limits and transfer check are specified in the dossier. These are reviewed authoring provisions, not observed pupil attainment. Overall B1/B3 remain separately pending until their other clauses and integrated tasks are accounted for.
