# India primary coverage: accepted scope and remaining work

Snapshot of accepted main `228e7421ed75e35e1a5a0fc91acb4884838ca086`. The agreed authoring scope is primary Classes I–V, including the relevant Foundational and Preparatory stage records. Physical Education duties and automatic VI+ expansion are excluded; health, safety, fine-motor work and social play contexts remain where relevant. State-board and CISCE coverage is not claimed.

**Comprehensive current NCERT/CBSE primary coverage is not yet established.** The selected baseline, older reference catalogue and distinct CBSE CT/AI supplement have separate denominators. The 21 CT/AI positions are not an overall CBSE denominator. Their goals, competencies and outcome positions overlap; they are not counts of independent skills. No national completion percentage can be derived from the current selected inventory.

The 2017 row is reference catalogue ingestion. The CT/AI supplement has two accepted Full source-competency alignments (C-3 and C-4); its other nineteen positions have not completed coverage review. These unlinked positions are unreviewed or applicability-dependent references, not proven skill gaps. They do not enter the 521-position selected NCERT denominator. Full is an authoring judgment, not learner attainment. Link counts are a separate axis and do not establish Full coverage.

The selected NCERT baseline contains 254 formal records (58 goals and 196 competencies) plus 316 selected 2025 positions. Its 1,164 associations reuse 246 existing topics. Neither 570 records nor 1,164 associations represent that many unique skills. The 21 selected CBSE CT/AI supplement positions similarly comprise three goals, six stage competencies and twelve grade-parent boxes, not 21 independent mastery claims.

The accepted baseline's 521 eligible dispositions reconcile as 444 Full, four Partial, 36 skill-with-context records, 24 conditional records, twelve contextual requirements and one Unverified source meaning. The 24 conditional records comprise eleven art-form, ten language and three graph-representation cases. Some have component links. The 26 unlinked eligible records are specifically five language-script bindings, eight art choices, twelve contextual observations and one music-source uncertainty. These categories must not be turned automatically into new topics or forced binary mastery claims. See the [accepted readiness report](ncert-primary-completion-readiness-2026-11-11.json) and [source disposition summary](ncert-primary-alignment-review-summary-2026-11-10.json).

Completion means that the current source/programme boundary is explicit, every selected in-scope requirement has a defensible whole-clause disposition, reused claims and any justified additions have appropriate evidence, and material/language/performance conditions are resolved or retained as honest configured conditions. Contextual requirements and exclusions remain legitimate dispositions; they need not become mastery topics. Unverified source meaning and unresolved mandatory clauses must not be labelled complete. Historical references may remain references where current applicability is not established. Accepted Git content must contain the reviewed result; downstream publication and learner attainment are separate.
