# India primary coverage: accepted scope and remaining work

Snapshot of accepted main `d4f5f80a26b94f355c27db07f45b4870e0fb913f`. The agreed authoring scope is primary Classes I–V, including the relevant Foundational and Preparatory stage records. Physical Education duties and automatic VI+ expansion are excluded; health, safety, fine-motor work and social play contexts remain where relevant. State-board and CISCE coverage is not claimed.

**Comprehensive current NCERT/CBSE primary coverage is not yet established.** The selected baseline, older reference catalogue and distinct CBSE framework have separate denominators. Their goals, competencies and outcome positions overlap; they are not counts of independent skills. No national completion percentage can be derived from the current selected inventory.

The selected NCERT baseline contains 254 formal records (58 goals and 196 competencies) plus 316 selected 2025 positions. Its 1,164 associations reuse 246 existing topics. Neither 570 records nor 1,164 associations represent that many unique skills. The CBSE 21 similarly comprise three goals, six stage competencies and twelve grade-parent boxes, not 21 independent mastery claims.

The accepted baseline's 521 eligible dispositions reconcile as 444 Full, four Partial, 36 skill-with-context records, 24 conditional records, twelve contextual requirements and one Unverified source meaning. The 24 conditional records comprise eleven art-form, ten language and three graph-representation cases. Some have component links. The 26 unlinked eligible records are specifically five language-script bindings, eight art choices, twelve contextual observations and one music-source uncertainty. These categories must not be turned automatically into new topics or forced binary mastery claims. The five unlinked script-dependent baseline competencies are Foundational LANG C-10.2, C-10.3 and C-11.2, Preparatory R1 C-3.4 and Preparatory R2 C-2.2. All ten conditional language records retain their actual-role requirements even where they already have component links. The separate 2025 named-English library has seventeen Grade I and seventeen Grade II positions; that does not resolve the Hindi/Urdu extraction boundary or choose national language roles.

The immediate proposed changes are two separate, unpublished CBSE competency slices: C-3 to existing Systematic Listing as a whole contribution, and C-4 to existing Choosing the right strategy as a component contribution. Each proposes one association in an existing India ages 8–11 stage context, no new topic and no claim or age change. Their material examples and authored assessments require semantic review and reconciliation with accepted main before publication. They are not included in the accepted counts above and do not promote any whole goal or graded parent box.

Completion means that the current source/programme boundary is explicit, every selected in-scope requirement has a defensible whole-clause disposition, reused claims and any justified additions have appropriate evidence, and material/language/performance conditions are resolved or retained as honest configured conditions. Contextual requirements and exclusions remain legitimate dispositions; they need not become mastery topics. Unverified source meaning and unresolved mandatory clauses must not be labelled complete. Historical references may remain references where current applicability is not established. Accepted Git content must contain the reviewed result; downstream publication and learner attainment are separate.
