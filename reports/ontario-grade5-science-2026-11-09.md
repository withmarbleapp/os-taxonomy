## Reuse and source-fit boundaries

- **Investigation operations:** existing independent variable control, repeat
  measurement, evidence use, prototype diagnosis and risk planning add real
  contributions. They are available skills, not an invented compulsory checklist.
  Appropriate protective equipment and actual safe performance remain explicit.
- **Coding:** existing variables store and update values, while input/branching
  and first-program claims process and execute them. Varied science-purpose
  storage/processing still needs an applied task; a game score alone is not the
  complete curriculum performance.
- **Health across subjects:** food selection/preparation and interpreted health
  messages support decisions. The digital-impact claim explicitly considers
  wellbeing. Complete social/environmental comparisons and supported mental-health
  connections remain Partial; a nutrient list does not prove them.
- **Body systems:** current vocabulary, digestive, circulation, breathing and brain
  claims supply varied organ functions. An existing inter-system claim directly
  links lungs, blood, heart, cells and waste return. Lifestyle evidence already
  connects several conditions/harms with affected body sites; no separate disease
  list or unlisted compulsory system checklist is invented.
- **Matter:** the state-properties claim tests more than names; physical change
  support combines no-new-material/recovery reasoning with deformation. Existing
  new-material evidence includes chemical signs. Observable bubbles or reversibility
  alone are not treated as an infallible physical/chemical classification rule.
- **Definition and thermal direction:** a particle model or a mass measurement is
  not automatically the complete matter definition. Heating/particle explanations
  contribute, while the complete absorption/release account remains explicit.
  These findings do not authorize new definition or freezing topics.
- **Structures:** existing force diagrams, contact/distance interactions and
  deformation are actual components. They do not by themselves certify internal
  structural-force reasoning or every external structural application. Weather
  and earthquake engineering supply real protective designs without a new hazard
  progression.
- **Energy:** existing forms, stored-potential examples, device conversions, natural
  resource classification and fossil-fuel warming are reused. Conservation and
  dissipation have complete later claims; those claims retain their current ages.
  Transfer labels alone do not demonstrate the full law or dispersal account.
- **Community knowledge:** verified conservation cases exist generally. They do
  not establish the named Indigenous community knowledges or ways of knowing.
  Appropriate community-authoritative resources remain necessary; Australian
  Country evidence is not relabelled.
