# Ontario Kindergarten mathematics, 2026 programme

The [task report](ontario-kindergarten-mathematics-design-2026-11-11.json)
accounts for all 36 mathematics records with eight prepared task families,
76 scoped component pairs and 39 contributing topics. It adds 68 pairs in
29 new contexts. Thirty-eight topics are reused; one shared estimation
operation and its original, untested teaching guide are added.

The [estimation review](quantity-estimation-review-2026-11-11.json) explains
the distinction between approximation before enumeration, exact counting,
subitising and rounding a written calculation. The existing numbers-to-120
topic already contains an estimation component alongside a wider representation
inventory. That overlap is recorded deliberately: its definition and guide remain
unchanged, and the independent estimation claim does not certify the compound
claim or transfer learner mastery. A known counted set rearranged without changing
its contents is a cardinality contrast, not evidence of failed estimation.

The task portfolio retains actual collection and recording of data, both object
and picture displays, two- and three-dimensional matching halves, changed-view
visualisation, and direct and indirect comparison of all four measurement
attributes. Counting to 100, full numbers-to-120 representation and older
orthographic drawing are not inferred from the Kindergarten tasks.

Full means the source duty has an actual prepared task and scoped contributing
claims. It does not certify a pupil, the entire inventory of a wider contributing
topic or its prerequisites. Contexts use conservative 4–6, 5–6 or 6–6 implementation
bands within the two-year programme; these are not official per-age milestones.
Existing authored topics, guides, dependencies and source catalogue are preserved.

Validation: generation, validation and generated-output checks passed; all 248
tests passed with no skips. Independent comparison preserved all 1,907 prior
authored topic definitions, 1,092 guides and 4,870 scoped contexts. No database or
learner-state writes were performed.
