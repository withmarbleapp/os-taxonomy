# Ontario Grade 6 electrical and flight review

This slice supplies ten freshly reviewed source/task closures in the 2022 Ontario
Science and Technology curriculum, for ages 11–12. The complete 42-record B/C/D/E
inventory distinguishes **10 fresh Full, 16 retained historical Full and 16
Partial**. A historical label is not a fresh review of every saved guide or source.

The electrical portfolio covers varied generation routes and actual series/parallel
applications. The flight portfolio covers environmental/societal impacts, properties
of air, force relationships and changes, and contrasting organism structures.
Complete original questions, expected evidence, near misses and changed cases are
bound to checked factual resources. These are authoring designs, not observations
that a learner completed a task or validated teaching interventions.

One reusable conceptual claim, **How flight forces work** (`mt_jJjjV2WtS6`), fills
an independently reviewed gap. Fourteen complete comparison cards and independent
witnesses show why generic buoyancy vocabulary, given-force diagrams, an actual
balanced-force investigation or mass/weight calculations do not supply the
aerodynamic lift/thrust model. The new claim is not a topic per force, organism,
expectation, country or difficulty level. Its two dependencies retain a basic
push/pull hard prerequisite and a supporting resistance soft prerequisite.

The new guide is self-contained when downstream publication excludes reports. Its
seven original cases carry explicit conditions and transfer questions; the organism
structure extension does not add a mastery gate to the force model. All four
canonical assessment criteria are mandatory, including a constant-speed turn,
bounded surface/airflow/propulsion/mass changes, an angle limit and contrasting
flyer transfer. NASA technical facts are distinguished from the guide’s **low
confidence** for untested teaching effectiveness.

The change adds 15 source associations and two exact Ontario 11–12 contexts. It
preserves all 1,906 existing authored cards and ages, all 1,091 existing research
records and their order/provenance, all 4,001 existing dependencies, and all 5,058
original context intervals and associations. Source catalogues, clusters and
archived research remain byte-identical. No existing buoyancy, diagram,
investigation, organism or later Mars/SETI claim is broadened.
