# NCERT Preparatory TWAU alignment

This dependent proposal reviews the final 32 accepted **The World Around Us** records: 25 formal competencies and seven curricular goals from NCF-SE 2023, first printed October 2024 (PDF 382–384). The official Preparatory stage is Grades 3–5 / ages 8–11, with attainment across and by the end of that stage; the interval is not an age-eight deadline. This is the selected NCERT national baseline, not every Indian board.

The [code-level report](ncert-twau-alignment-2026-11-10.json) proposes **29 Full authoring dispositions and three mixed skill/contextual-orientation dispositions**, with **114 component associations across 54 existing topics**. It adds no topic, guide, dependency or authored-claim revision. There are 46 new IN contexts and eight extensions of an existing exact IN 8–11 context. All old contexts and codes remain; 32 existing topics' generated age envelopes change as a consequence of the complete scoped contexts.

Full means that the source duties are accounted for by an original task programme with explicit conditions. It does not mean a child passed, every linked whole topic was mastered, or an observed classroom intervention was effective. The [31 task families](ncert-twau-protocols-2026-11-10.json) include seven broad-goal compositions: each names its full child protocols and requires an independent connected-case explanation. Tags or counts cannot substitute for those duties. The programme spans the stage: its concrete example panels do not impose a one-sitting quota or turn every example, optional representation or art form into a universal separate mandate.

## What the tasks actually require

Local observing includes ethical actual or dated records, equipment use and comparison, sky identification and questions/predictions before new evidence. Making requires an actual independently made object for a purpose, with a functional check. Institution work explains roles and functioning of family, school, bank, post, market and a locally verified Panchayat, using different representations. Past/present work uses attributed accounts and surviving evidence, including technology's effects and the possibility of different genuine perspectives.

Systems work connects natural and made components to daily support: water supply/cycle and river flow, living cycles, food/material journeys, transport, communication and household electricity. The science portfolio retains actual safe inquiry, data construction, conclusions, new predictions and improvements, observable matter cases and four energy-transfer pathways. Engineering includes a functional build/test/improve loop. These are representative foundations for later subjects, not mastery of every later course or an advanced nuclear-energy/quantitative-law bundle.

Safety work includes the complete source hazard set in adult-approved discussion/planning cases, actual short labels and slogans, and safe enacted roleplay. It does not stage smoke, injury, electrical faults or flood. Children are not assigned technical treatment, firefighting or rescue. Protection tasks never require trauma disclosure, blame a child who froze, or promise that preparation guarantees safety.

Care and resource tasks retain actual sharing/maintenance, appropriate recurring plant/bird/animal support, resource mechanisms, access/opportunity/work/shelter reasoning and common-space practices. The three mixed rows retain a contextual ethical orientation alongside concrete knowledge/actions. No pleasant mood, religious agreement, forced affection or moral-compliance score is required. Minibeast handling is not inferred from observing or supporting wildlife; the separate existing whole handling claim is not linked here.

Map tasks actually interpret line plans, draw useful sketches with symbols and directions, and read city/state/country maps. Inquiry outputs include actual tables, graphs, diagrams and use/build of a simple classification key, plus own oral and written whole-card checks where relevant. The source's creative alternatives do not become compulsory mastery of every art form; language/script and access are declared for the actual material.

## Reuse, resources and boundaries

Every contribution is a direct component of an explicit task; prerequisite edges are not extra alignment tags. The structured guide cautions preserve four distinctions: the Moon can orbit Earth while both travel around the Sun; map interpretation is not own model construction; resource sorting is not actually obtaining and combining information; and systematic observational recording need not be a controlled experiment. During isothermal melting, added energy need not increase particle speed; positional language uses an explicit reference frame without making deferred advanced viewpoint/dual relations universal gates. A narrow inherited diagnostic does not certify the whole broader card. Inquiry evaluation is method-specific: a control for a controlled comparison/test, consistent recording for observation, and reliable source choice/limitations for research. These cautions do not edit an edge, guide, age gate or canonical claim. Complete existing cards, assessments and hard paths remain separate from a source-panel result. Country-specific Australian community-change and environment cards were rejected as unqualified Indian substitutes. Existing water-cycle overlap and temperate-season overlap remain candidates for deliberate later claim review; this proposal does not merge them or remove their requirements. The forecast topic's inherited four-season check uses a separately attributed temperate case, not a universal Indian calendar.

The institution dossier uses [RBI's school material](https://www.rbi.org.in/financialeducation/content/01SCHOOL20042018.pdf) for conceptual saving/banking only and [MoPR's Panchayat information](https://panchayat.gov.in/en/e-gramswaraj/) with its [state-specific revenue index](https://panchayat.gov.in/en/%E0%A4%93%E0%A4%8F%E0%A4%B8%E0%A4%86%E0%A4%B0/). It imports no old account eligibility, rate, financial transaction or universal levy. [CBSE's 2019 student handbook](https://www.cbse.gov.in/cbsenew/documents/Handbook_for_students.pdf), printed 169–171, supports adult-led preparedness and inclusion; it is supplementary context rather than current legal/technical-response advice or a new NCERT catalogue source.

The resource dossier compares three real efforts: [Modhera's dated solar-plus-storage project](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1866334), [community water programmes](https://www.pib.gov.in/FactsheetDetails.aspx?Id=150467&lang=1&reg=3) and [ICAR's named-village millet/soil demonstration](https://www.icar.gov.in/en/icar-iimr-hyderabad-scientists-demonstrate-millet-technologies-sustainable-agriculture-during-khet). Actual care tasks accompany source-based mechanism explanations. It asserts no universal yield, zero-water crop, potability, exact environmental gain or medical benefit. [Madurai district's harvest account](https://madurai.nic.in/festivals-and-events/) is one attributed environment/practice case, not every Indian community's calendar or a compulsory ritual. All resource roles and limits are also structured in the protocol file. These are original untested designs; old teaching-guide verification flags and bytes remain unchanged.
