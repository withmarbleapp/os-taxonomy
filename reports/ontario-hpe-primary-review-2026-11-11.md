# Ontario primary HPE: source-fit review

All **267** primary source records have been read. The current proposed portfolio
accounts for **182 records**: **56 Movement Competence records (C1/C2)**,
**71 Active Living records (B1/B2/B3)**, **16 Healthy Eating records** **20 Personal Safety records** and **19 Human Development records** in Grades 1–6. It contains **182 Full**
authoring dispositions, **401 component pairs** and **55 topics**. The remaining
**85 records** (other Healthy Living and integrated SEL) are pending
whole-claim/task qualification, not excluded or automatically missing topics.
The [record ledger](ontario-hpe-primary-review-2026-11-11.json) preserves every
selected identity and that distinction.

The source is [Ontario HPE 2019](https://www.edu.gov.on.ca/eng/curriculum/elementary/2019-health-physical-education-grades-1to8.pdf).
Its overall and specific expectations are formal grade-end duties. Examples and
prompts illustrate implementations at an appropriate complexity, rather than a
checklist of every sport or technique. The source treats development as
age-related, not age-dependent, and requires access and capability adaptations.
The exact Ontario grade contexts are not universal birthday or fitness norms.
The 88 higher-grade records remain in the catalogue and outside this slice.

The [original task programme](ontario-hpe-primary-task-programme-2026-11-11.json)
retains actual balance/weight transfer, varied travel and controlled landings,
sending/receiving, possession while moving, activity components, functional game
features and applied choices. Grade 4's low-height landing, younger one-/two-foot
take-off cases and later relation to others/equipment remain explicit. One held
pose, a lucky catch, a diagram or a spoken tactic name is insufficient.

Existing claims are reused deliberately:

- The six movement primitives retain their full performance and changed-case
  checks. Source-specific foot/equipment receiving is assessed directly; hand
  catching does not automatically establish it.
- Catching already includes secure retention during modest travel. Combined with
  actual locomotor control and the bound route/object conditions, it supports the
  possession portfolio. A dribbling option does not become a universal requirement
  or a new topic for each sport.
- Stop, Think, Then Choose supplies a genuine decision component. An actual
  modified game still requires identifying options/consequences, choosing with a
  reason, acting and responding to a changed situation. A maths/study strategy
  card is not silently broadened into a sport claim.
- The older category tasks use actual played-game rules/objectives and the
  source's declared functional model. Complete classification/counting and
  count-out/cardinality checks remain separate whole-card assessments, not new HPE
  numeric requirements. The model is not portrayed as a universal taxonomy.
- Actual rule-following, fair roles, listening and turn-taking remain. A group's
  outcome or an agreeable mood does not certify any member's competence.

The programme includes Movement-context SEL applications. It does not infer all
six A1 source duties across the still-pending Healthy Living contexts and the complete cross-strand portfolio.
No upset, frustration, enthusiasm, private disclosure or positive feeling is
manufactured for an assessment. Adapted source participation is not automatically
recorded as every fixed canonical motor pattern.

The Active Living portfolio now also provides actual varied participation,
personal enjoyment/access reflection, realistic daily options and barrier
responses, daily activity with both warm-up and cool-down, physical and mental
benefit reasoning, safe exertion assessment, repeated personal observations and
implemented interest-based goals. The source's daily 20-minute opportunity is a
programme condition, not a global fitness norm. The Grade 5 selected-component
and Grade 6 four-component longitudinal duties remain distinct. Blank templates,
concept answers or an unimplemented plan do not constitute a child's evidence.
Personal understanding does not require enjoying every activity, reporting a
positive mood or disclosing private health/family circumstances.

The [new fitness-component concept](ontario-hpe-fitness-component-claim-2026-11-11.md),
the [bounded minor-care concept](ontario-hpe-minor-care-claim-2026-11-11.md), the [food-allergy knowledge concept](ontario-hpe-allergy-claim-2026-11-11.md) and the [medicine authorisation concept](ontario-hpe-medicines-claim-2026-11-11.md) are the four new shared claims/guides in the cumulative HPE work so far. Old authored cards and dependencies remain unchanged; Human Development makes the 12 explicitly registered factual guide corrections below. Earlier
scoped requirements remain exact; standards and compatibility age envelopes are
generated. A Full label is an authoring judgment about available tasks and
conditions, not a claim that a child performed them or an intervention is validated.

Safety tasks now retain actual controlled behaviour, equipment/space checks, current environmental and medical-support arrangements, stop/report responses and the minor-care knowledge cases. Both actual 2025 Rowan booklets and current provincial policy were read. The actual school/board procedure, local environmental information and individual support must be bound by the responsible adult at use. Neither a provincial booklet nor a prepared case certifies local compliance, diagnosis or clearance.

Integrated SEL and Healthy Living remain next. Human
Development/Sexual Health records stay in scope; a school's exemption procedure
is a runtime policy condition, not an invented user exclusion.

Generation, validation, generated-artifact checking and the required test suite
are checked at each coherent checkpoint. Source identity, exact protocol and
condition bindings, child-portfolio closure and scoped-pair guards reject wrong
valid protocols, omitted preparation/recovery, reduced daily opportunity,
concept-only longitudinal substitution, unimplemented plans, forced enjoyment,
deleted pairs and wrong-age duplicates. All **240 tests passed**, alongside generation, validation and generated-artifact checking. The Active Living delta preserves all **1,903 authored cards**, **1,088 guide payloads and sequence**, all **4,896 prior contexts**, and every old dependency/catalogue/archive/cluster byte. It adds **27 contexts**, extends **21 exact existing bands**, and adds **126 pairs** (three fitness-component pairs already existed in the preceding checkpoint). Four generated age ends expand; no starts are lowered.

The safety association checkpoint also passed all **252 tests** and required generation/validation checks. It preserves all **1,904 prior authored cards**, **1,089 guide payloads and sequence**, and **4,924 contexts**, with no edge/catalogue/archive/cluster changes. Exactly **8 contexts** are new, **9 exact bands** are extended and **34 pairs** are added; the minor-care pair already existed. One generated age end expands, with no start lowered.

The eating portfolio uses the actual current Canadian guide, nutrient/food-group distinctions, safe hunger/thirst/fullness information, actual food-origin/processing/environment facts, oral-health choices, an attributed recipe and actual habit/goal and message-critique tasks. No deprivation, forced tasting, appetite/weight judgment or private family-budget disclosure is required. Source-specific cases do not certify every clause of a broader reused topic.

The unchanged nutrition-label claim now has a reviewed exact Grade 5 context at ages 10–11; its old Australian 11–12 context and full authored requirements remain. The Canadian source task uses actual per-serving/ingredient/nutrient-warning information. Its separate whole-claim transfer uses [original two-column practice panels](assets/ontario-hpe-programme/label-comparison.svg) and the [numeric cases/answer key](assets/ontario-hpe-programme/label-comparison.json): per-100-g comparisons and given ratings in an explicitly Australian-context exercise. The hypothetical ratings are not calculated commercial HSR scores, Canadian features or assurances about an entire diet, portion or allergy safety. All panel arithmetic and the full rendered page were checked; no extra Ontario curriculum obligation or automatic topic mastery is inferred.

The eating checkpoint passed generation, validation, generated-artifact checking and all **259 tests**. All **1,904 existing authored cards**, **1,089 guide payloads and their sequence**, **4,932 contexts** and every old dependency/catalogue/archive/cluster byte remain unchanged. ## Personal safety and help

Twenty further source duties are accounted for by explicit original tasks, including actual guideline creation, risk-choice/action/reflection, concussion knowledge and school procedure, consent, safe support and inclusion. The [programme](ontario-hpe-primary-task-programme-2026-11-11.json) retains varied settings, fictional unsafe cases and actual benign communication practice. It does not certify a child’s diagnosis, emergency care, babysitting readiness or ability to prevent abuse. The one independently reviewed food-allergy knowledge component is documented separately in the [claim report](ontario-hpe-allergy-claim-2026-11-11.md).

Current [Ontario bullying guidance](https://www.ontario.ca/page/bullying-we-can-all-help-stop-it) informs the local source task: power/context matter, not every one-off disagreement is bullying, and a harmful incident does not need to repeat before help. The inherited repeated-intent definition remains unchanged and is not presented as the complete Ontario definition. Actual [safe-adult information](https://kidshelpphone.ca/get-info/how-identify-safe-adult) supports choosing help; no real service contact, unsafe-image handling or personal disclosure is an assessment action. Full older card and hard-chain evidence stays separate from a source-specific component.

The Personal Safety checkpoint passes all **272 tests** and required generation/validation checks. It preserves all **1,905 existing authored cards**, **1,090 guide payloads and sequence**, every **4,942 old context** and all dependency/catalogue/archive/cluster bytes. It adds **29 contexts**, extends **8 exact bands** and adds **40 pairs** (the allergy pair already existed). **3** generated age ends expand with no start lowered; no old authored claim, guide or edge changes in this association slice.

## Human development and relationships

Nineteen further specific duties have source-fit portfolios, including actual anatomy and reproductive-process knowledge, private-care routines, consent, identity, puberty support and non-graphic discussion of sexual-media representation. There is no body inspection, private identity or medical-history requirement, forced positive feeling or exposure to explicit media. Grade 5 process knowledge is assessed directly and cannot be inferred from a puberty component.

The complete unchanged reproduction claim is qualified at one exact Ontario ages 10–11 context, retaining its older 12–13 context and all four evidence clauses. Its separate whole task includes both systems and their functions, fertilisation, gestation/birth, the placenta/cord and maternal-substance effects. These whole-claim checks extend beyond the minimum Ontario source task and are not new universal Ontario duties. The [original programme](ontario-hpe-primary-task-programme-2026-11-11.json) contains the actual cards, arrow-model answers, near misses and transfer.

Twelve repeated guide strings receive a narrow [factual clarification](ontario-hpe-reproduction-whole-fit-2026-11-11.json): substance exchange occurs across normally separate circulations; oxygen can diffuse while nutrients use different mechanisms. This retains the functional-exchange claim, requires no advanced transport vocabulary and avoids treating the placenta as a perfect barrier. No old source-verification flag is upgraded. The original glucose/alanine study abstracts support this correction, not a claim of child-intervention efficacy. Old guide revisions must remain available downstream; no learner equivalence, reset or transfer is authorised.

Human Development validation passes generation, validation, generated checks and all 285 tests. The exact boundary retains all 1,906 old authored cards, all 4,972 old contexts and all 4,001 edges; only the 12 registered factual guide strings change. There are 21 new contexts, 10 exact-band extensions and 36 new component pairs, with no new claim, guide or edge in this source slice. Derived envelopes change for three reused topics while every prior scoped interval remains intact.
