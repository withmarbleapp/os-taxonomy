# Ontario Grade 6 Social Studies: source-fit completion proposal

This dependent proposal accounts for all 46 mandatory Grade 6 expectations in the
actual 2023 course served by Ontario's current official curriculum API. It covers
ages 11–12. The [current ledger](ontario-grade6-social-current-ledger-2026-11-11.json)
is the current code-by-code result; the earlier bounded dossiers retain the facts,
whole-claim comparisons and tasks reviewed at their respective checkpoints.

All 46 positions are reviewed: 43 Full authoring designs and three explicit
local-resource Partials, A3.1, A3.10 and the affected A3 overall. Full means that
mandatory knowledge and applicable resource/performance conditions are accounted
for in the specified tasks. The two own-community packets are not yet bound to an
actual target locality; a concrete preparation protocol and worked case do not
fill that requirement for every learner. It does not mean learners have passed those tasks, every
real-world outcome has been demonstrated, or this branch has reached the default
branch or staging. The final delivery is through the supervised integration PR,
not a separate publication from this branch.

The inventory is 92 source/topic associations across 18 topics: 16 reused claims
and two previously reviewed additions. Counts describe associations, not a
completion metric or 92 distinct skills. No Grade 7/8 scope is added.

## What the task designs retain

The community strand uses the actual existing early-contact/Métis, period-life,
New France, cultural-renewal and territory repertoires rather than creating
community or grade clones. Particular people, source purposes, chronology and
historical versus current contexts remain distinct. The physical/built/social
feature task assesses both selected public images **of** Canada and a particular
community's expressed identity **in** Canada; it does not turn a personal self-map
or promotional text into a national opinion survey.

Both inquiry strands require actual questions and suitable multiple sources,
primary/secondary evidence where specified, meaningful print **and** digital map
analysis **and** construction, source/evidence evaluation and original cited
communication. The global-response cases distinguish reported outputs, potential
fund matching and actual outcomes. No irrelevant source-mention chart is created.

Own-community change and territorial identification use explicit adult-prepared,
locally verified resource protocols. A worked Toronto or other public case does
not automatically certify every learner's community. Actual delivery must supply
the correct own-community records and preserve the specified source, modality and
performance requirements. A missing delivery resource is reported as such;
fictional success or absent classroom observations are not substituted for it.

The inclusion task distinguishes institutional/legal steps and community agency
from proof of universal lived inclusion. National principles, particular treaty
references and continuing diverse identities do not imply uniform personal
beliefs, automatic consent, present legal title or language proficiency. Optional
Native Languages remains outside this integration; Indigenous content within
Social Studies remains fully within scope.
