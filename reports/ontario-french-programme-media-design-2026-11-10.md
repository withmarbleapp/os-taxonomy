# Ontario Extended/Immersion French: purpose, media and language choices

This [66-record design](ontario-french-programme-media-design-2026-11-10.json) covers seven additional expectations in each of nine primary Extended/Immersion courses, plus the three Immersion Grades 4–6 sentence-convention expectations. It adds 114 component pairs on 12 existing topics, with 55 new Ontario contexts and 11 extensions. No topic, guide or authored mastery claim is added or rewritten.

All 594 retained primary expectation bodies, embedded examples and 216 nested teaching tips were read against the actual courses. The [publisher's guidance on printed page 17](https://www.edu.gov.on.ca/eng/curriculum/elementary/fsl18-2013curr.pdf) requires alternatives of similar complexity, while treating examples as illustrative. The report distinguishes early familiar-language tasks, later academic/personal contexts, actual unfamiliar listening words, both verbal/non-verbal responses, evidence-backed responses and supported beginning voice. These are performance conditions on shared claims, not difficulty levels.

| A1.4 | React to actual French oral media and their presentation; express a supported opinion in words and a non-verbal form, with actual text/presentation evidence in later profiles. |

| D1.4 | Create an original French opinion with a clear view and reason, with deliberate word/style choices. Beginning voice retains teacher support; later work develops connected relevant ideas. |
| D1.5 | Plan, compose, edit and publish actual French writing with purposeful visuals: information plus diagram/caption, a poster and a picture-book page. Revise both text and image after feedback. |
| D1.6, Immersion 4–6 | Construct and expand complete French sentences and produce/edit fresh connected work. Check actual spelling/accents, word order, agreement, punctuation and intended time, with feedback and meaningful revision. |

Original French inputs, models, references and production tasks are included. Prepare the actual checked sound, print, tables, posters and illustrations before use; a resource label or model copied as attainment is insufficient. No old English result or different French corpus automatically certifies these performances. Illustrative vocabulary, grammar and products do not become exhaustive inventories, universal speed/length thresholds or compulsory emotional reactions. The other 396 Extended/Immersion records remain under qualification, including their community applicability conditions.

Two prerequisites are corrected for intrinsic reasons that also apply to the complete English claims:

- `mt_LMX-nZETLM` → `mt_FGCFUCqJBB` becomes soft. A writer can make and justify precise/vivid written choices, produce plain and purposeful versions, and justify a verb/adjective without first rehearsing dialogue or varied sentence openings aloud. All target evidence remains required.
- `mt_AHXePdicRf` → `mt_AvJMWQbDsr` becomes soft. Complete short writing-plus-image products, including an informative paragraph/diagram/caption and revision of a picture-book page, do not require dividing an extended text into several themed paragraphs or justifying paragraph breaks. The complete multimodal operation remains intact.

These are positive whole-target counterexamples, not a reason to weaken tasks for French or age. Basic Informational Writing remains a hard prerequisite for multimodal production; complete facts/definition/conclusion and other readiness claims are checked separately. The full picture/text prerequisite for multimedia analysis also remains. Broader Greek/Latin vocabulary analysis, author-argument evaluation and rhetorical-technique claims were considered and rejected as unnecessary whole matches; they are not narrowed or replaced with easier clones.

This raises proposed source-fit French designs from 132 to 198 of the 594 retained primary records. It does not certify learner attainment or readiness, change private learner state or publish to staging. All prior authored topics, guides, source identities, contexts and intervals remain preserved; only the two documented edges change semantically.
