## Reuse and conservative boundaries

- **Biodiversity:** the existing species-variety/keystone claim already explains
  benefits, resilience and loss cascades. Same-species variation and survival
  explanations also exist. Neither is replaced with a new Ontario biodiversity
  topic. The complete multi-level diversity and within/among-community account
  remains Partial; the later broader resilience claim is not lowered.
- **Interactions:** colony roles, symbiosis, food webs and habitat engineering are
  real contributions across interaction categories. Merely listing them does not
  certify an integrated explanation of sustained diversity.
- **Classification:** existing grouping, unfamiliar-organism evidence and keys
  supply further classification. No unlisted compulsory advanced taxonomic-rank
  scheme is invented.
- **Local invasive case:** an authoritative CFIA reference localizes existing
  invasion/harm and biodiversity reasoning. This supplies an example resource,
  not a new provincial topic. Harmful invasion is distinguished from harmless
  introduced status; declining abundance is not automatically species extinction.
- **Electricity:** current and static charge explanations, conductor tests,
  conversion/device work and circuit-component functions already exist. Basic
  configuration vocabulary contributes to series/parallel comparison, while the
  complete common-use account remains explicit. The later full circuit-rules
  claim retains its age and meaning.
- **Applied performances:** a planned local conservation response is not an action
  already carried out. Private energy choices do not automatically establish
  school advocacy. These are performance conditions, not missing mastery IDs.
- **Flight:** drag, weight, force balance and structure/function arguments are
  actual components. They do not establish every lift/thrust relationship or
  machine/organism control application. Upthrust terminology is not silently
  equated with a complete aerodynamic-lift explanation. No new flight topic is
  requested in this batch.
- **Independent overall scope:** D1 concerns environmental aviation effects;
  D1.1 concerns societal effects and local/global perspectives. The overall remains
  independently Partial rather than inheriting an answer from its single child.
- **Space:** existing objects, mass/weight, geometry and observation technologies
  supply substantial knowledge. General astronaut/needs lists are not substituted
  for actual space-condition responses. The later Mars/SETI claim is not lowered
  for one matching human-needs clause. Checked space-impact cases remain explicit.
- **Community resources:** generic farming, generation sources and conservation
  knowledge do not establish culturally specific agriculture or Canadian community
  impacts. Appropriate sources and relevant case judgments remain required;
  Australian cultural evidence is not relabelled.
