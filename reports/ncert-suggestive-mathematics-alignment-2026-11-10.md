These are **suggestive, contextualisable source positions**, not formal mandates or official numbered outcome codes. Their generated identifiers preserve printed positions and grade assignments. Full means the claims and explicit task/resource design account for the position; it does not report learner attainment, equate prompted success with independent mastery, or convert the source into an exhaustive compulsory syllabus. ## Scope and age provenance

The supplied source selects Grades 1–2 within the broader Foundational stage. The pinned NCF-SE framework explicitly places **early primary Grades 1–2 at ages 6–8** (PDF page 211). This slice therefore adds one exact **IN 6–8** context per selected topic. It preserves every existing formal Foundational **3–8**, Preparatory **8–11**, and other jurisdiction interval. No separate 6–7 / 7–8 grade deadlines are inferred.

Every printed source grade remains in the ledger. A broad contextual age interval neither moves these selected positions into preschool nor sets the same performance deadline at every age. Assistance, language/representation and local materials are retained as assessment conditions. Actual assessment must use those conditions and the whole topic rubric; an association is not an automatic award of mastery.

## Reuse decisions

- **Counting and arithmetic:** group sizes, descending order and representation choices are explicit task cases of existing operations. They do not justify new number-range, counting-step or difficulty-level claims. Concrete grouping, group count, objects per group and total are distinguished.
- **Patterns:** assisted missing-term work remains assisted. Repeating/additive structures are translated between objects and symbols rather than inferred from one guessed next term.
- **Geometry:** physical attribute comparisons separate length, covered area and occupied solid space. Straight/curved distinctions and drawing use existing shape analysis, sorting and construction; the larger ray/angle bundle is not imported. Tracing and shadow identification have concrete verification tasks.
- **Informal measures:** the earlier body/game-unit positions retain their approximate or non-uniform context. The separate same-size-unit accuracy claim is preserved. The later positions explicitly use equal informal units along actual paths and check estimates with a common reference. Container-capacity comparison is not mislabelled as liquid-volume conservation.
- **Time and direction:** actual duration measurement is distinct from clock-face reading. The selected hour/day/week relationships use the relevant time-unit and calendar claims, not a misleadingly broad title about calendar facts. Four direction words are checked as precise positional vocabulary with a supplied north reference; grid coordinates and independent navigation are not added requirements.
- **Money and language:** local INR material is a resource condition of the existing currency-value operation. Number words or digit forms in one language/script do not establish knowledge of another. The numeral dossier uses the document's printed decimal forms and does not invent a regional script target.
- **Problem solving:** explaining two simple methods and checking work reuse the early representation/explanation and checking claims. Games must actually require quantification; generic persistence or merely joining a game does not substitute. Formal problem formulation and contextual confidence/value requirements remain separate follow-up decisions.

## Locally varying seasons without new country claims

The existing four-temperate-season cards are **not** used to certify summer/winter/monsoon distinctions. Their original claims and contexts remain intact; their overlap is a separate review. Instead, the complete weather-data claim and local-calendar claim support a source-fit task.

The learner collects/selects dated local observations, records them in a **table**, constructs a **graphical display**, and describes/compares typical conditions across periods before ordering the local seasonal cycle. A simple single-observation pictogram/count scale keeps the complete operation within early primary; there is no mean/rate calculation or meteorological-instrument gate. Reading a prepared graph alone would be insufficient.

The dossier identifies official IMD multi-week/monthly Delhi records as an example input, with station/date traceability and missing-data rules. The [1991–2020 Palam climate table](https://mausam.imd.gov.in/newdelhi/mcdata/palam2.pdf) provides an adult context check, not a learner average-calculation task. A contrasting [official Chennai climate account](https://imdpune.gov.in/library/public/CLIMATE%20OF%20TAMILNADU.pdf) prevents treating Delhi's wetter-period timing as universal. Local material binding must be accurate; the dossier does not claim a school-specific packet, learner weather diary or field study has already been completed. No monsoon mechanics or advanced storm-system topic is added.
