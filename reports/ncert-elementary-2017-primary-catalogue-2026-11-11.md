Its continued listing on the [official current learning-outcome index](https://ncert.nic.in/learning-outcome.php) does **not** establish that every 2017 item is unchanged or superseded under the 2026 rollout. **Individual-outcome applicability in 2026 remains Unverified.** The [17 March 2026 textbook advisory](https://ncert.nic.in/pdf/announcement/notices/Advisory_ncertbooks.pdf) establishes release of newer textbooks; it does not supply an item-by-item equivalence or repeal table. The [CBSE outcomes-approach circular](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/Circulars/2020/34_Circular_2020.pdf) supports the outcomes approach without certifying every old item unchanged in 2026.
