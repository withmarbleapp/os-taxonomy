### Original worked illustration and independent assessment

This is a **2025–26 historical pairing illustration**, not a claim that a current school is in Madhya Pradesh or that the pair continues in 2026–27. That session’s annex paired Madhya Pradesh with Bihar. The actual [Madhubani district government craft account](https://madhubani.nic.in/handicraft/) describes line-based forms, flat colour and repeated infill associated with Mithila painting. Use the attributed account as the cultural input. A teacher may supply a checked artist example by link with its attribution; no unseen photograph is claimed as read or copied here. The classroom work below is an original study informed by those features, not an authenticated traditional artist’s product or exhaustive certification of the art form.

Give each group ordinary paper and safe pencils/crayons. Each learner first draws an original simple plant, bird, fish or other suitable everyday motif, outlines it clearly and tries two different line/shape infills on separate small practice areas. The group chooses how to arrange its own motifs within a border, using a deliberately selected repeated unit, then actually draws/colours the composition. Do not require religious imagery, make caste/gender assumptions about the pupil, or use potentially unsuitable traditional pigment preparation as a child task. Each learner explains one visible art choice and one source-supported cultural fact, distinguishing those from their own invented design.

Integrate the selected maths/language learning: identify the actual border’s smallest repeating unit, predict the next two positions and explain the choice; then produce an original caption or accessible oral explanation describing the design and attributing its researched inspiration. A copied border/name alone does not demonstrate either the artistic decision or the underlying concept. The teacher checks each learner’s participation, the visible line/colour/infill choices, accurate source attribution, the chosen subject explanation, and a reasoned reflection on a revision. These are stated criteria, not aesthetic taste, compulsory cultural pride or professional craft quality.

For an independent changed case, replace the border’s unit or one motif/material constraint and ask each learner to make and explain a new appropriate contribution without copying the teacher’s completed solution. A group product cannot certify an absent individual operation. Ask what changed, what source-supported feature remains and which details are the learner’s original invention. For I/II, reduce the composition to a short shared picture and one actual personal mark/pattern/explanation with suitable support; do not impose an older pupil’s product. Before applying this to another session/state/art form, verify the actual pair and replace the cultural material with an attributed appropriate source while preserving the inquiry→artistic production→subject assessment→reflection operation.

This contract provides implementable project authoring with actual local/source configuration gates. It does not assert that the illustrative pairing is current, a school has provided the project, pupil work has been assessed, or anything has been sent to KALASETU. No new general topic, source Full judgment or association is inferred from the contract.
