# EVS43: original source task registry

Each source position has an independent task/transfer contract. Referenced examples are selected teaching routes; they do not mandate every listed form, unit, language or institution for every child. Whole claims and broader paths remain independent. Actual contextual care is retained honestly; no learner attainment is claimed.

## evs2017-g3-01 — Observe and compare local plant features
ncert-elementary-learning-outcomes-2017:IN-NCERT-LO-2017-EVS-G3-LO01

Actually perform the complete selected existing original protocols: `local-living-diversity`.

**Compare local living things.** Make ethical local observations or use reliable dated records for plants, birds and other animals. Compare shape, sound where appropriate, food, growth/life stage and habitat. Record specific features, relate needs to conditions and explain a supported comparison; full organism groups/life-cycle diagrams remain separate whole-card checks. Unknown wildlife is observed at a distance; no required capture, forced touch or fabricated growth/sound. Independent changed assessment: Compare a new local pair or microhabitat and explain how an uncertain identification can be checked. Positive evidence: Specific differences and similarities are tied to observed/attributed evidence across all required dimensions, acknowledging unavailable records. Near miss: A generic naming worksheet, adult-imposed guessed call, giving every plant the same requirements, or using one day’s size difference as a growth measurement.

Actual local/material/access key: Bind actual safe local leaf/trunk/bark observations and source-approved smell only where appropriate; teacher key must preserve observable feature not generic plant naming.. ## evs2017-g3-07 — Classify observed features
ncert-elementary-learning-outcomes-2017:IN-NCERT-LO-2017-EVS-G3-LO07

Actually perform the complete selected existing original protocols: `safe-inquiry`, `findings-representations`.

**Actually investigate a question.** Let the learner formulate/choose a specific question and choose observation, a one-change safe comparison/test or reliable research. For a direct observation/test, use appropriate simple equipment and describe/compare actual samples; research instead uses actual identified sources. Make an appropriate prior prediction where the question supports one, gather data/information and answer using evidence. Do an independent OR a group option without treating either as universally required. Check practical risks and handle/clean any equipment safely. For a controlled comparison/test, explain the relevant control; for observational inquiry explain consistent recording, and for research explain source choice/reliability. In each case state a relevant limitation and improvement. Complete whole-card equipment/testing checks separately rather than infer them from research alone. A surprising result is legitimate; no manufactured confusion/distress. Independent changed assessment: Use a changed sample/question and justify the chosen method. Explain a control for a controlled comparison/test, consistent recording for observation, or source choice/reliability for research, with an appropriate limitation/improvement. Positive evidence: The method fits the question; actual data, observation and interpretation are distinguishable and support a bounded answer. Near miss: A memorised fact presented as an investigation, changing everything at once, adult-only equipment use or a post-hoc “prediction”.

**Show findings, not invented results.** Use actual inquiry/local-source records. Organise a headed table, produce a graph/pictogram, a labelled observation diagram and both use and build a suitable simple classification key across the programme. Present the finding through at least two declared accessible modes including own oral AND written explanations with appropriate access/support across the programme; optional poem/play/skit remains a valid source alternative, not a compulsory art-form AND requirement. Distinguish what was seen from its explanation, give a supported conclusion, a new prediction and a method improvement. No automatic arbitrary-language mastery. Records may come from systematic observation rather than a controlled experiment; no one-variable-test requirement is inferred for all recording simply from an inherited hard path. Independent changed assessment: Present the same evidence to a new audience/format and check whether the meaning and uncertainty survive. Positive evidence: Representations reflect actual records and the conclusion answers the question with evidence/provenance; the learner can explain a changed-case prediction and limitation. Near miss: Neat invented data, graph with no source, memorised poem unrelated to findings or an interpretation called a direct observation.

Actual local/material/access key: Actual sorting key must include selected sensory features; no tasting unknown material or compulsory preference claim.. Supplied models remain models, and actual independent performance is recorded honestly. Source-only success awards no entire broader topic or ancestor.
