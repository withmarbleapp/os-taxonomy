The [finite circular ledger](india-cbse-current-implementation-applicability-2026-11-12.json) identifies all 77 unique entries observed in the official 2026 academic circular index, with bounded operative-body reviews for all 77 entries. These comprise 71 newly read bodies and six retained bounded core reviews; no index-only entry remains. Its coverage is neither every CBSE directive nor a comprehensive India denominator.

## Implementation contract

For each applicable school programme, record its issuer and exact source edition, school year, operative dates, region, eligible class/age, responsible adult, selected language or other permitted route, actual resources and any required annexes. Preserve the difference between a direction to provide a school opportunity, a pupil choosing to enter, a suggested alternative activity, and a curricular learning requirement. An opportunity can be required of the school while individual participation remains conditional.

Before delivery, verify the current call and relevant annexes, prepare the actual age-appropriate material and access arrangements, and keep adult administration separate from pupil performance. Source-authoring completeness does not assert school registration, programme implementation, learner participation, submission, positive feelings or attainment. No registration, public posting, message or learner-data handling is performed by this review.

Closed calls remain part of dated implementation history. They do not become indefinite pupil obligations, and a calendar entry for a later cycle does not carry forward the old cycle’s word limits, rubric, eligibility or portal rules without an applicable call. New dates require a new applicability check.

## Source-content and implementation gates kept separate

The finite circular-index body screen is complete at this date and boundary. It does not finish the separate selected current textbook/material review, establish each 2017 outcome’s current equivalence, identify the target language of the Hindi-script role rendition, verify the provisional Urdu extraction, resolve the musical-scale wording, or establish a currently applicable art-project edition. These are source-content, programme-binding or source-meaning questions, with their existing dispositions retained. Conversely, actual event entry, selected local language, staff qualification, facility checks, regional access, programme-specific annex administration and real pupil performance are implementation conditions. They remain explicit without requiring every optional programme or named language simultaneously. The observed 77-entry index is not asserted to exhaust every historical circular, notification or future directive.
