Only the **Learning Outcomes column** is included. The separate Suggested Pedagogical Processes column, stage introductions, Classes VI–VIII and all other subject sections are excluded. Nested dash operations, examples and continuations remain attached to their printed top-level parent. The first Class I bullet contains two joined clauses and remains one source position; its later pattern diagram also remains attached. Pages 76 and 81 add no outcome-column positions. All 11 table pages were visually inspected; independent bounding-box and separately cropped Poppler reconstruction matched all 49 private texts ignoring whitespace.

Identifiers such as `IN-NCERT-LO-2017-MATH-G1-LO01` are **generated source-position references, not official NCERT outcome codes**. The document-level source is `ncert-elementary-learning-outcomes-2017`; these older positions do not replace or recode the accepted 2024/2025 identities. Restricted wording and PDF bytes stay private under [PROVENANCE](../PROVENANCE.md). The pinned official document is 16,481,419 bytes with SHA-256 `18024190d8d8a7b0516bf6bcab14dc209f4751b91acb0b0c952e2c27b4ac3317`.

The 2017 publication presents classwise learning checkpoints/assessment benchmarks and labels adjacent pedagogical processes as suggestive. Its continued listing on the [official current learning-outcome index](https://ncert.nic.in/learning-outcome.php) does **not** establish that every 2017 item is unchanged or superseded under the 2026 rollout. **Individual-outcome applicability in 2026 remains Unverified.** The [17 March 2026 textbook advisory](https://ncert.nic.in/pdf/announcement/notices/Advisory_ncertbooks.pdf) establishes release of newer textbooks; it does not supply an item-by-item equivalence or repeal table. The [CBSE outcomes-approach circular](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/Circulars/2020/34_Circular_2020.pdf) supports the outcomes approach without certifying every old item unchanged in 2026.
