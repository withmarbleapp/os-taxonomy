# Grade 2 contemporary community resources: bounded task design

The [machine dossier](ontario-grade2-community-resource-review-2026-11-10.json) records actual sources, whole-claim contributions, positive/near-miss/transfer tasks and remaining conditions. It starts at `9d015d2` and makes no new data edits or Full upgrades.

[QIA’s community account](https://www.qia.ca/who-we-are/communities/), [Iqaluit’s municipal services](https://iqaluit.ca/departments/corporate-services/municipal-services) and [Nunavut Parks’ current local account](https://nunavutparks.com/nunavut_parks/iqaluit-kuunga-sylvia-grinnell-territorial-park/) support a specific contemporary Inuit-community case, including modern institutions, piped and trucked water, and particular regional features/recreation. Iqaluit is diverse; no case represents every Inuit person.

Dated primary climate summaries and the [Singapore weather agency](https://www.weather.gov.sg/climate-climate-of-singapore) allow a seasonal comparison. [BCA’s passive-design account](https://www1.bca.gov.sg/resources/newsroom/incorporate-passive-design-in-buildings-to-reduce-air-conditioning-the-straits-times-forum/) and the [dated Iqaluit infrastructure account](https://www.canada.ca/en/crown-indigenous-relations-northern-affairs/news/2020/03/building-resilient-communities-in-the-north-and-mitigating-impacts-of-climate-change.html) support actual adaptation relationships rather than inferred uniform lifestyles. [PUB’s water account](https://www.pub.gov.sg/Public/WaterLoop/OurWaterStory) and [NParks’ wildlife resource](https://avs.nparks.gov.sg/wildlife/encountering-wildlife/otters/) add particular facts.

The proposed tasks require actual own-community application, like-for-like comparisons and source attribution. Supplied evidence can make distant places familiar; it does not silently broaden a familiar-map claim into global geographical knowledge. The existing climate claim needs its separately proposed seasonal correction. Its full three-climate comparison is not certified solely by these two cities.

Map/photo resources, a complete selected-needs packet and the coherent inquiry protocol are still being completed. The dossier keeps those conditions explicit rather than upgrades from link counts. These are authoring tasks, not a study requiring learner records or evidence anyone passed them.

The integrated B2 proposal now uses the actual 1991–2020 monthly temperature summaries: all twelve calendar-month means per city, with a relevant below-freezing-mean category key (Iqaluit eight, Singapore none). That chart answers a real climate-pattern question; it does not count source mentions, predict daily weather or infer cultural identity. The protocol includes question/method choice, map construction and analysis, evidence evaluation, a supported conclusion, bounded prediction, method improvement and communication. Newly proposed contributors remain subject to exact scoped association review.
