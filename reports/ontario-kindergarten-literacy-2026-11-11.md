The [task report](ontario-kindergarten-literacy-design-2026-11-11.json)
accounts for all 21 literacy records using seven actual task families and 24
unchanged topics. Its 56 scoped component pairs add 55 pairs in 23 contexts.
No topic, teaching guide or dependency is added or semantically changed.

The declared phonics packet includes the actual taught correspondences, separate
unseen reading and spelling words, and two original matched texts with meaning
checks. Letter naming, actual formation, oral sound analysis, independent reading
and encoding remain separate performances. A brief task does not certify an
inherited topic's complete phoneme, handwriting or publishing inventory.

The creator portfolio uses identified actual portions: Richard Van Camp's
publisher-displayed *Little You* opening, the beginning of Julie Flett's first
interview answer, and Kenojuak Ashevak's complete museum-displayed *The Enchanted
Owl*. These were read or viewed directly. The official linked definition includes
visual texts. No complete-book review is claimed; the interview is not the
*Birdsong* story, and artwork does not replace the separate independent written
reading task. Museum imagery is linked rather than reproduced.

The original bag illustration was rendered and visually checked. It supports a
specific picture/word comparison: its star adds a visible detail absent from the
practice sentences. Creator-resource access and the learner-relative demand of
a read-aloud remain explicit delivery requirements. If the actual resource is
unavailable, its applicability condition must be retained.

All generation, validation and generated-output checks passed; all 250 tests
passed without skips. Independent comparison preserved all 1,908 prior authored
cards, 1,093 guide records and 4,899 scoped contexts. These are source-authoring
results, not observed learner attainment or database publication.
