# CBSE CT/AI supplement (Classes III–V): appropriate methods

This local proposal adds one association from the CBSE CT/AI supplement (Classes III–V), Preparatory C-4 to existing topic `mt_mQcWGh02no` (Choosing the right strategy), with a **component** role. The [ledger](cbse-method-choice-alignment-2026-11-12.json) records the selected competency's Full authoring disposition, material bindings and authored assessment. It changes no topic meaning, evidence, assessment prompt, prerequisite or age interval.

The existing India context, ages 8–11, is retained as the Preparatory stage window with stage-end attainment as the reference. The competency applies once across Classes III–V. Grade III examples do not create grade-specific standards or identical deadlines at each grade. The topic also includes harder arithmetic and other representations; this source contributes to that broader claim rather than redefining it.

The accepted [method-choice protocol](ncert-preparatory-mathematics-assessment-protocols-2026-11-10.json) tests choosing and justifying mental, written and estimation approaches. Correct arithmetic alone does not establish this source requirement. The current [Grade III teacher handbook](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/CurriculumMain27/CTAI_Pri3TH_2026-27.pdf), PDF 9 / printed 8, supports independent attempts and discussion of approaches; that guidance informs delivery without replacing evidence of the learner's choice.

Two current material contexts were visually checked in the [student handbook](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/CurriculumMain27/CTAI_Pri3SH_2026-27.pdf) and corresponding teacher solutions: Chapter 6 question 3, student PDF 28 / printed 27 and teacher PDF 45 / printed 44; Chapter 12 question 6, student PDF 45 / printed 44 and teacher PDF 79 / printed 78. The first supplies a convenient small difference; the second requires tracking intermediate quantities. The printed questions ask for results. The authored protocol supplies the separate strategy-choice prompts; it is not presented as additional official wording.

Ask why a mental method is convenient for the small difference, then let the learner choose a reliable method for the transfer states and explain how it avoids lost updates. A well-justified mental approach remains acceptable; written work is not forced for its own sake. For an original transfer, use four accounts starting at 48 counters each, then transfers of 17 and 23 between specified accounts. Ask for a rough total before exact working and for a reasonableness check afterwards. The ledger gives the exact setup and expected result. This variation is an authored assessment, not another catalogue standard.

The selected competency is Full for authoring coverage when assessed with these explicit probes. A selected answer, a copied teacher method, speed or an unexamined routine is insufficient. This does not certify learner mastery, the whole broader topic, any whole goal, any graded parent box or any other selected CT/AI supplement record.
