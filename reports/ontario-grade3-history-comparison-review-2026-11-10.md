# Grade 3 historical comparison task review

The [dossier](ontario-grade3-history-comparison-review-2026-11-10.json) resolves A1.1, A3.3, A3.4 and A3.8 through seven component links extending existing Ontario ages 8–9 contexts. The approved period-life and treaty knowledge claims supply particular facts; the existing land-purpose claim contributes the purpose/need distinction. No new claim, interval, dependency or guide is added.

The reviewed tasks compare specific everyday-life evidence, explain settlement setting and goods/services, describe period challenges, and distinguish cooperation from conflict in actual intercommunity relationships. They preserve each source’s date, place and perspective. Different First Nations, Métis, Inuit and settler cases remain distinct; a colonial agreement does not imply fair implementation, and a later school policy is not moved into 1780–1850.

A1.3 remains pending. Its then/now task must account for roles **and** challenges in at least two actual Indigenous community cases with supported contemporary facts. One actual community plus a fictional modern school/home scenario does not satisfy that qualifier. The proposed A1.3 link has not been authored.

The current source-fit ledger separates these four resolutions from the remaining historical inquiry, identity, locations, relocation, settlement impacts and overall goals. This is authoring coverage, without any claim of learner attainment or intervention testing.
