# NCERT Preparatory Mathematics alignment

This review covers the five curricular goals and nineteen competencies in the formal Preparatory Mathematics tables of NCERT's *National Curriculum Framework for School Education 2023*, printed October 2024 edition, PDF pages 267–268. It is the NCERT national baseline, not a claim about every Indian board. The [record ledger](ncert-preparatory-mathematics-alignment-2026-11-10.json) and [original assessment protocols](ncert-preparatory-mathematics-assessment-protocols-2026-11-10.json) contain the code-by-code decisions.

The official Preparatory window is ages **8–11**, Grades **3–5**. Each context uses that complete stage interval once, with stage-end attainment as its reference. It does not create identical deadlines at every age, change the existing Foundational 3–8 contexts, or create topic difficulty levels.

## Reuse and assessment design

The protocol dossier supplies original tasks, near misses and transfer checks. It is assessment-design evidence, not a new editable taxonomy or reproduced curriculum wording.

- **Local numeral representation:** lakh/crore labels and comma placement are a source-fit representation of existing decimal place value. The unchanged large-whole-number claim is assessed with a supplied chart and examples through one crore, within its ten-million range. This does not add a national number-reading clone or assert an unlimited numerical range.
- **Common units:** informal/standard measurement, inverse count-to-unit-size reasoning, precise unit communication and appropriate unit choice jointly support a handspan disagreement and common-reference task. A second task transfers the reasoning to supplied cup sizes. A new social phrasing of that reasoning does not justify another topic.
- **Patterns, puzzles and listing:** recurrence examples, arithmetic grids and constrained pair choices have explicit tasks over general pattern, operation and systematic-listing claims. A correct isolated calculation or an unfiltered product count is insufficient. Named example formats do not automatically become mastery claims.
- **Broad goals:** combined number, measurement and problem-solving protocols explicitly check integration across their relevant operations. Their Full judgments are not inferred solely from descendant-code links.
- **Measurement:** actual event timing remains separate from clock reading, known-endpoint duration calculation and endpoint finding. Conversion uses the accepted surviving generic claim; retired conversion profiles are not restored.

The source-fit choice protocol uses `mt_mQcWGh02no` alone. A second existing strategy topic has overlapping wording and an age-level prerequisite to that topic; this review neither multiplies the association nor silently cleans up those existing claims. That overlap remains a separate claim-review item.

## Three justified new claims

| Topic | Claim boundary and decisive mismatch |
| --- | --- |
| `mt_ZtACVRIqmM` — Length stays the same when an object is moved or bent | Preserve length along an unchanged cord/ribbon despite altered endpoint separation; distinguish bending from stretching or cutting. Indirect length comparison and rigid congruence do not establish this prediction. A parcel-wrapping task provides everyday transfer. |
| `mt_dLJu-KoJAM` — Liquid volume stays the same during ordinary pouring | Predict unchanged liquid volume despite a changed waterline, with no loss, addition or other changed conditions. Separate contents from container capacity. Measuring a volume, discrete cardinality and physical mass conservation are different claims. Serving juice provides everyday transfer. This is not a universal physical volume-conservation law through heating or mixing. |
| `mt_iFQ6cOJr6e` — Development and spread of zero and positional notation | Explain a bounded historical-development and transmission account and its present significance. Using place value, or knowing general cultural legacies, does not establish that historical knowledge. The existing Medieval Times domain accommodates this global knowledge-transmission account without a country tree. |

The new claims have explicit evidence, near misses, transfer tasks and five **soft** dependencies. No hard gate is inferred from a plausible teaching sequence. Historical chronology and place-value models may be supplied in an assessment.

Historical content is grounded in the original scholarship on the transmission of Indian arithmetic through Arabic-writing scholars and Latin adaptations, and the scholarly distinction between a placeholder and zero as a number. It avoids a sole inventor, contested first dates, and any implication that India invented binary circuitry or every subsequent technology. See [Allard, 1991, *Arabic Sciences and Philosophy*](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0957423900001508) and [MacTutor's history of zero](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/HistTopics/Zero/). A modern decimal record or calculator display is a bounded impact example.

These are original mastery claims and assessment designs justified by the source and the documented reuse failures. No teaching-efficacy study is claimed. The structured teaching-research coverage honestly adds these three to its missing-record list; all existing research records remain unchanged.
