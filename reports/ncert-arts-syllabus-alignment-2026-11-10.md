## Reuse and one bounded concept

There are 108 direct component pairs across 42 topics: 41 reused claims and one new shared concept, **Sound and noise in context** (`mt_u-Cp7JuK9R`). Twenty-six form-specific candidates remain conditional rather than flattening visual, musical, movement and dramatic alternatives into universal simultaneous requirements.

The noise concept fills a narrow knowledge gap: existing vibration, pitch, loudness, vocabulary, justified-opinion and perspective claims do not entail recognising noise as sound or explaining why the same sound can be wanted in one supplied situation and interfering in another. The new concept has no dependency edges and one original, untested, low-confidence guide. It does not broaden an existing Physics or personal-preference claim.

The source table asks for the distinction but does not define it. This proposal therefore declares a **limited, defensible everyday interpretation**, supported by actual government technical explanations, rather than claiming a publisher-defined technical meaning. It does not teach that all music is wanted, all machines are noise, quiet always means wanted or loudness alone determines the classification. No loud or irritating exposure, hearing diagnosis, sound-meter course or advanced waveform criterion is introduced.

## Actual source duties, beyond a topic label

The tasks retain actual sound-cued movement recognition and recall, coordinated movement, deliberate lines across materials, clay making, body sound, small-instrument output, own card/poster design and printing/impressions used in a card or bookmark. Body-only output cannot close the instrument position; a drawing cannot replace an embodied dramatic tableau; ready-made texture sorting cannot replace actual printing or clay manipulation.

Story completion, group drama, relevant listening/speaking, productive vocabulary, organisation, classification, generalisation and transfer keep their actual duties. The complete unchanged scripts-and-poetry claim still requires its script, poem and improvisation portfolio. Sorting includes its separate counting/category-order checks. The existing materials-origin claim retains its inherited fair-test assessment separately; that is not relabelled a mandatory NCERT origin operation. Canonical whole-claim assessment and the narrower curriculum duty are distinguished throughout.

Local nature and cultural examples need accurate attribution and appropriate context. Actual care/protection and responsible material use remain tasks, not generic praise. Source non-disturbance care does not automatically prove the whole minibeast handling claim: a suitable safe handling demonstration is separately needed for that claim. Unknown organisms are not handled to create evidence. Family/cultural cases may protect privacy through suitable supplied examples, without compulsory religious participation or a fixed national language pair.

The guide review accounts for 66 complete candidate/closure cards, 37 hard edges and 31 available guides. Earlier full readbacks were reused only against unchanged records, with additional complete material-guide readback. Historical confidence/verification flags are not fresh efficacy evidence. Existing authored cards, guides and edges are preserved; component links do not certify an entire inherited chain or any child’s feelings.

## Completion boundary

All 56 source positions retain their identities, printed shared-stage scope, selected view and overlap relationships. Broad or alternative-form tasks stay conditional until their implementation binding is made; no new user choice is required to integrate the framework. Enjoyment and mixed engagement stay visible without forced enthusiasm or a learner score.

The physical-source musical scale-progression phrase remains explicitly **Unverified** in the cumulative ledger. The new Music claims and this sound/noise interpretation do not resolve that separate source-meaning issue. No source wording, private evidence or learner state is included in this PR.

The review correction for the listening/observation family supplies an actual simple-equipment task: inspect two safe known samples, describe specific details, compare a similarity and difference from those observations, and respond to a peer’s observation. Its explicit panel/evidence guard rejects deleting that task or its comparison operation; discussing an adult-supplied observation is insufficient. This applies to the observation panels, not every listening task.
