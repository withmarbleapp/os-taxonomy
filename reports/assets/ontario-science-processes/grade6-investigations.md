# Grade 6: dispersal models, circuits, flight and a space-exploration model

Use the [common process records](shared-process-records.md), including independent planning, variables/controls, actual repeats, source assessment, risk review and communication. Read the selected Ontario 2022 biodiversity/electrical-energy/flight/space content passages with source identity. The following are original model investigations, not validated interventions or pre-filled scientific results.

## B — A dispersal model and biodiversity question

Question: does changing the wing shape of the same safe paper seed model affect where it lands? Use large paper/card models and a low fixed release point in a clear indoor area; no climbing, sharp points, thrown objects, fans directed at faces or actual seed dispersal outdoors. Keep paper amount, release height/orientation, release method and environment comparable. Record actual landing distance/area for at least three trials per design and explain variation. The result tests model flight/dispersal under stated conditions; it does not prove that a species survives, that greater distance always helps, or that biodiversity/stability increased. Research the actual biodiversity question using the selected source account and explain that extra biological evidence would be needed. Changed case: a larger model with the same wing outline; identify changed mass/area and test rather than assume scale has no effect.

## C — Safe electrical-energy transformation

Question: how does changing the approved number of identical small lamps in a safe classroom circuit affect the observed lamp output under the selected arrangement? The adult supplies an intact low-voltage educational kit, its instructions and compatible components, checks maximum ratings and teaches the safe connection procedure. No mains supply, loose button cells, damaged batteries, deliberate short circuit, heating element or unsupported battery combination. Compare an approved single-lamp and two-lamp arrangement while keeping the specified supply, lamp type and connection plan comparable. Record the actual visible output by a stated consistent observation method; do not invent a numerical brightness/energy reading or guarantee a contrast when the kit regulates current. Repeat safely and stop for warming/damage. Draw and explain the actual arrangement, where input energy comes from and the observed transformation; brightness alone is not a complete efficiency measurement. Changed case: a different rated lamp or a changed branch arrangement; consult instructions and plan a new safe comparison rather than reuse an unsupported result. Design a simple model signal for an actual partner, with visible-message and safe-operation criteria, test two approved designs, obtain feedback and revise.

## D — Controlled paper flight

Question: does changing one wing feature alter the distance or landing position of a safe paper glider under this method? Use rounded paper models, a marked low release point and a clear indoor lane, no pointed projectile, forceful launch, people/animals as targets or flight from heights. State a consistent gentle release method and the changed feature; keep paper amount, initial position and measurement method comparable. Use at least three trials per design, record actual outcomes and compare spread as well as a summary. Release variation is a limitation, not permission to remove a disappointing flight. Compare three initial sketches against distance/control criteria, build and test two, use actual partner feedback and improve/retest. Changed case: a different release method; distinguish a confound from evidence that the wing modification alone caused the outcome. Results do not establish every aerodynamic force by direct measurement or certify an aircraft design.

## E — Landing a model exploration payload

Question: which model landing design protects the same classroom payload under the same low controlled release? The science context is planning a space-exploration landing instrument, with a selected source account of exploration technologies. Use a lightweight non-fragile model payload with an adult-approved observable indicator (for example whether a loosely fitted paper flag remains in its marked holder), a low fixed release, tray and clear zone. No raw egg, glass, thrown object, high drop or live organism. Compare at least three sketches against two criteria (retains the indicator and remains in the marked landing zone) and material/time constraints; construct two solutions, test repeatedly, record actual indicators and landing positions, obtain partner-user feedback and revise/retest. Keep payload, height, orientation, surface and release method comparable; discuss limits. The original Earth-classroom model does not reproduce lunar gravity, atmosphere or actual spacecraft safety. Changed case: a different surface or payload position; identify what needs retesting. Research distinguishes what the exploration source actually establishes from what the classroom test establishes.

## Integrated evidence

The pupil poses at least three own investigable questions across all four contexts and selects methods appropriately. At least one independently planned experiment includes a reasoned hypothesis, independent/dependent variables, three relevant controls, repeats/sample choices and a reviewed hazard/control. A shared written/multimodal project contains attributed selected source notes, actual observations, model limits and answers supported by specific records. Use a meaningful table and suitable bar/line/other display only where the actual variable/question warrants it; a prepared result is never relabelled as the child's measurement. Whole broad instrument/graph inventories remain separate checks.

Communicate to the partner-user and to another investigator, using scientific vocabulary and a form suited to purpose. Give a specific conclusion, degree of trust, changed-case prediction and relevant improvement; compare prediction with outcome. Revise misleading claims, labels or explanations after partner feedback. The common source-assessment contrast and established actual safe-tool procedures remain part of the programme. A demonstration of model landing, paper flight or circuit interpretation alone is insufficient for the integrated research/experiment/design duty.
