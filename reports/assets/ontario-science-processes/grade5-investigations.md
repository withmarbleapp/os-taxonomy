# Grade 5: body responses, matter, structural loads and energy

Use the [common process records](shared-process-records.md), including independent planning, actual repeated records, variables/controls, source limits and design feedback. Four content contexts are required in this programme. Read the selected Ontario 2022 body-system/matter/structure/energy content passages with their source identity; they frame the questions, not validate these original methods.

## B — A safe model of moving air

Question: what happens at the opening of an intact hand-operated bellows when its enclosed flexible chamber changes volume? The adult provides a clean, intact, low-force classroom bellows or suitable respiratory teaching model, a large light paper indicator held safely near its opening and a stable marked support. No mouth blowing, shared mouthpiece, biological sample, latex exposure, improvised pressure vessel, sharp assembly or air directed at faces. The pupil observes and draws the chamber and the opening; compare no operation with the same gentle adult-approved stroke, keeping indicator position, device and stroke method comparable. Record actual indicator movement/no movement/unclear over repeated trials; do not label an inferred airflow amount as a measured volume. A changed indicator distance needs another comparison.

Use the selected [NHLBI account of the muscles used for breathing](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/lungs/body-controls-breathing) to relate this bounded mechanical observation to moving air, while explicitly identifying the model's limit: human lungs are not a hand-squeezed bellows, and the model does not demonstrate gas exchange, every respiratory structure or interactions among all body systems. A shared source project distinguishes the device observation, the source's body-system account and the pupil's inference. The investigation involves no exercise, fitness test, personal physiology or private health record.

The selected NHLBI muscle section was read as actual text (updated March 24, 2022); no illustration or linked media was inspected. An original short resource summary is: breathing involves muscular changes around the lungs that help move air; the lungs do not enlarge independently. Ask which part of our model is only an analogy, and why squeezing a bellows is not an anatomical account of a person. No disease or specialist nervous-system inventory is introduced.

An optional ordinary comfortable observation may compare breathing before and after an already-permitted accessible everyday action, but it is not needed for this programme and no pupil is required to exert themselves or disclose body data. It cannot establish fitness, diagnosis, a norm or the superiority of one pupil over another. A voluntary seated/observer route is always available. No breath holding, pulse touching, maximal activity or deliberate heat/air-quality exposure is used. No Physical Education coverage or personal exercise-plan attainment is claimed.

## C — Dissolving under a controlled method

Question: does stirring change the time for the same amount of an adult-approved soluble classroom material to disappear visibly into room-temperature water? Use clean unbreakable cups, safe spoons, equal measured quantities and a timer, with allergy/skin checks and no tasting. Choose one changed variable and state the observed endpoint before starting. Keep water amount/starting temperature, solute amount/grain type, vessel and observation method comparable; record stirring method and actual times, including incomplete or uncertain endpoints. Make at least three comparable trials per condition when practical and explain variability rather than select only the fastest. Visible disappearance does not establish destruction of matter or, by itself, a new substance. Changed case: larger grains at the same mass; identify a new variable and test before reusing the original numerical prediction. No hot water, cleaning chemicals, fumes or reversibility shortcut is introduced.

## D — A low-load structure

Question: does changing the support spacing affect the measured deflection of the SAME safe model beam under the same small central load? The adult supplies a safe flexible strip, stable low supports, a small approved load and a ruler; test close to a tray/table surface, with hands clear and stop before damage. Keep beam material, dimensions, load amount/placement and measurement point alike, changing one support spacing. Record actual unloaded/loaded positions by the same method, repeat and retain the measurement resolution/uncertainty. The deflection is observed; internal tension/compression is a separate qualified model explanation, not directly measured here. For an actual partner's small display need, compare three sketches against two criteria and constraints, construct two alternatives, test under the same safe load, record strengths/weaknesses and revise/retest after feedback. Changed case: a different load position; a new test is needed rather than claiming every beam has the same loading pattern or capacity. This is not a real-building certificate.

## E — Slowing temperature change

Question: which model wrapper changes the rate at which equal amounts of mildly warm water approach room temperature? An adult supplies a safe comfortable water temperature within school instructions, checks it before handing over, and provides intact unbreakable containers, thermometers and safe wrapping materials. Children never boil, heat or carry hot water. Compare equal water amounts/initial temperatures, identical containers, place and agreed reading times, changing wrapper type only; include an unwrapped comparison. Record actual temperature/time values, at least three readings per condition, appropriate units and instrument resolution. Outcomes depend on the setup; do not guarantee a wrapper keeps temperature fixed or claim it creates energy. Compare controlled results with a selected source explanation; losses across the outer boundary are not a violation of conservation. Changed case: different initial temperature or a lid change; identify what needs a new comparison. A partner's need to keep a model drink comfortably warm supplies criteria/constraints, two tested designs and feedback/revision, without drinking the water or generalising to every commercial cup.

## Integrated evidence

The pupil independently plans at least one safe experiment, explaining independent/dependent variables, at least three relevant controls and why the method addresses the question. Across the four contexts, generate at least three own questions and choose observation, test or published-source research appropriately. A shared written/multimodal project contains actual firsthand records, attributed selected source notes and distinctions between observation, model and inference. Plot actual time-series where useful, without invented or mandatory straight-line results; broader graph/equipment inventories remain whole-claim checks. Report a specific evidence-backed conclusion, prediction for a changed case, relevant improvement and degree of trust based on sample, repeats and errors. Adapt vocabulary, form and explanation for the partner user and a fellow investigator, revising content/labels after feedback. The common source-assessment contrast and actual hazard-control checks remain required.

## Science scope and the excluded Physical Education programme

The mechanical-model investigation above is self-contained science. It does not require the excluded Physical Education programme or any not-yet-integrated HPE report. The optional ordinary observation is not a requirement for exercise, fitness assessment, a personal activity plan, medical history or a physiological norm. Acute observation and longitudinal assessment remain different operations; neither is inferred from the model.
