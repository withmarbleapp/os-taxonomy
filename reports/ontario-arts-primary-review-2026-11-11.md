# Ontario primary Arts: source-fit authoring review

This proposal accounts for all **300** Arts expectations in Grades 1–6: **72
Dance, 72 Drama, 78 Music and 78 Visual Arts**. Each record has an original task
duty, exact-grade concept panel, named protocol and component associations in the
[record ledger](ontario-arts-primary-review-2026-11-11.json). The proposed authoring
disposition is **300 Full**. This means the source requirements are accounted for
in the task programme; it does not mean a child has performed them or that an
intervention has been validated.

The source is [Ontario's 2009 Arts curriculum](https://www.edu.gov.on.ca/eng/curriculum/elementary/arts18b09curr.pdf).
The review uses its official expectation records and all 24 primary grade/area
concept panels, together with its creative and critical processes. Grade-end
requirements remain distinct from illustrative examples. Grades 7–8 remain in the
catalogue and are outside this authoring slice. This branch depends on the combined
primary integration proposal; it has not reached the default branch or a database.

The [task programme](ontario-arts-primary-task-programme-2026-11-11.json) contains
17 reusable families and 24 concrete grade panels. The 72 overall records retain
all their exact-grade child duties. The programme includes actual production,
response and context work; naming an element or attaching a topic is insufficient.

The programme uses three newly curated shared claims from its bounded checkpoints:

- **Reading and recording staff notation** (`mt_Gy9nCgUjlD`) uses actual conventional
  staff recovery, recording, diagnosis and transfer. Early reversible icon tasks
  do not certify it. Its direct source contexts remain Grades 4–6.
- **Mixing and adjusting paint colours** (`mt_-STxLMqeqF`) requires an actual mixture,
  sample comparison, deliberate adjustment and material/target transfer. Selecting
  ready-made paint or composing an image does not establish that operation. Later
  wheel, complementary and tertiary duties remain explicit grade tasks.
- **Drawing one-point perspective** (`mt_JmErYQaKgp`) uses a chosen horizon/point,
  coherent depth construction, repair and changed-view transfer. Its one minimal
  direct association is Grade 6 D1.3, ages 11–12. The older parallel-projection
  claim remains unchanged and is not a substitute or new prerequisite. The
  [original comparison tasks](assets/ontario-arts-programme/onepoint-tasks.svg)
  ([PNG](assets/ontario-arts-programme/onepoint-tasks.png)) contrast valid conventions
  and a supplied inconsistent candidate; neatness or photorealism is not a gate.

There are **483 component pairs across 16 topics**, including the earlier two
bounded checkpoints. A component is not a whole-expectation certification or proof
of its topic's complete mastery. The ledger deliberately avoids incidental
feedback, cultural-diversity, group-work, markmaking and repeating-unit associations
where a specific duty does not require that contribution. The new one-point claim
is retained as a child task within the Grade 6 overall portfolio without inventing
another direct overall association.

Full existing cards and their hard paths were reviewed. Actual script, poem and
improvisation remain separate checks; a mathematical repeat core is not a finite
musical form; a source-permitted visual or movement response alone does not
certify a whole spoken-presentation claim. Cultural and social affect is not
inferred from an artwork label, smile or compliant performance. No fixed liking,
private family disclosure, universal colour/emotion meaning, copied ceremony,
fixed vocal range or body/vision norm is imposed.

All earlier authored claims, guide payloads, dependencies, source inventories and
context requirements are retained. Compatibility standards and age envelopes are
generated from the complete scoped contexts. Adding a source band does not change
an old claim's evidence or authorize mastery transfer, learner resets or
publication. The three new guides are original low-confidence proposals grounded
in the stated sources; historical guide flags retain their existing meaning.

Generation, validation, generated-artifact checking and all **223 tests passed**.
The consistency guards bind all 300 source identities, exact protocols and grade
panels, overall child closures, original resources and scoped associations;
wrong-valid protocol swaps, missing children/pairs, duplicated contributions,
early staff claims and wrong-age duplicate contexts are rejected. Local
preservation comparison against the citation checkpoint retains all 1,901 old
authored cards, 1,086 old guide payloads and 4,786 old contexts; only generated
compatibility fields change. This final portion adds one approved one-point
claim/guide, 59 contexts and 471 pairs, extends 21 existing contexts and changes
zero dependencies. Six existing global age envelopes change only as a derived
projection. The earlier staff/paint checkpoints supply the other 12 pairs.
