## Remove an unnecessary hard prerequisite

The single removed edge was Natural and made materials (`mt_i8AGTuk4fJ`) → Testing Materials for Uses (`mt_e3-_toGuWf`). The latter requires independently designing controlled property tests, recording results and using those results to justify a material’s use. The former classifies materials/fibres and traces their origins. A property test does not establish manufacturing provenance.

Two independent hypothetical witnesses make the distinction reviewable:

- A learner can correctly classify broad natural/manufactured examples and cotton/wool/polyester/nylon labels, trace selected raw origins and reject the woven-cotton misconception, while failing to control variables in a separate waterproofing test.
- A learner can independently conduct a fair test and justify a raincoat material from measured results, while not knowing fibre origins or the sources of manufactured materials.

The relationship is removed rather than automatically softened: a thematic connection alone does not justify a dependency. The other 4002 edges, both other soft consumers of Testing Materials, the two existing soft supports for Natural and made materials, and all AU/IN fair-test associations remain unchanged. No graph cycle or topic identity change is introduced. The local 6–7 context is separately supported by the complete unchanged three-criterion task using spoken labels and concrete source examples; independent reading, percentages and chemical formulas are not extra learner gates.

Removing an unnecessary prerequisite can affect future planner eligibility for the unchanged classification claim. It does not establish learner mastery, transfer estimates or authorize learner-state changes. This PR performs no DB, staging or publication operation.
