# Ontario Language Grades 1–4 — historical reconciliation

This review recovers the evidence behind the accepted Language associations at
`4a7ba5a0476c7009694e2b11bfacdfc7343d1062`. It closes a public documentation gap:
Grades 1–4 already had applied authoring work. The absence of public per-grade
reports did not establish missing curriculum coverage or missing topics.

All **292 catalogue codes and 969 distinct topic–code pairs** reconcile with the
applied history after its accepted corrections. Current selected descriptions,
evidence and assessment prompts match the corresponding applied versions.
The official 2023 source was read again on 10 November 2026: all 292 main
expectation descriptions match the historical records after HTML/whitespace
normalization. This change adds **no topics or associations** and changes no
claims, dependencies, ages, research records or curriculum data.

The [code-by-code ledger](ontario-language-grades1-4-reconciliation-2026-11-10.json)
records every code, its current holders and complete accepted context bands,
historical semantic judgment, child rollup and retained conditions. Official
wording, private audit identifiers and source-response hashes are omitted under
[the publication policy](../PROVENANCE.md#ontario-elementary-documents--codes-and-source-links-only).

## Evidence and limits

The fresh checks establish source transcription, accepted association identity
and continuity of the selected mastery claims. They do not repeat all historical
pedagogical decisions or turn inherited Full labels into a new clause-by-clause
certification. The historical judgments remain separately labelled in the ledger.
No learner evidence, classroom delivery or mastery was examined or certified.

The reconciliation uses the final applied holder lists, not earlier design
previews. Public acceptance is traceable through [Grade 1 PR #75](https://github.com/withmarbleapp/os-taxonomy/pull/75),
[Grade 2 PR #76](https://github.com/withmarbleapp/os-taxonomy/pull/76),
[Grade 3 PR #77](https://github.com/withmarbleapp/os-taxonomy/pull/77) and
[Grade 4 PR #80](https://github.com/withmarbleapp/os-taxonomy/pull/80).
Official source identities are preserved in
[Language, 2023](https://www.dcp.edu.gov.on.ca/en/curriculum/elementary-language)
and the grade links in the ledger. CMS copy aliases are not used to infer the
grade: the published item identity and its grade membership were checked.
Linked guidance entries were distinguished from numbered expectations and were
not counted as additional codes.

many contexts carry them. Holder counts are per grade and cannot be summed as
unique taxonomy topics. All 244 specific codes have recorded contributions.
Overall expectations can be accounted for through their children without a direct
association. An unlinked overall code is therefore not counted as a missing skill.
Historical `whole` holder labels concern whole-claim fit; one such holder does not
necessarily complete every clause of a multi-part expectation.
Context bands are reproduced from accepted data, including any historical bounds
above age 12. They are preserved evidence, not new authorization to extend this
primary-school review or widen those contexts.
