# Ontario HPE: food-allergy and sensitivity knowledge

Proposed shared concept `mt_rVjbdwUJHG` accounts for a bounded common-trigger/reaction model, with one Grade 2 D1.2 component association at ages 7–8 and no dependency. It distinguishes a known allergy from a familiar non-allergic intolerance while preserving uncertainty about unexplained symptoms. It does not certify diagnosis, treatment or any actual learner performance.

Full food-hygiene, nutrition, digestive and immune-defence claims do not require this knowledge. The [original cases and limits](ontario-hpe-allergy-claim-2026-11-11.json) distinguish varied possible triggers, the same food with different known conditions and a changed unknown case. A healthy-food rating or a friend eating a food does not establish individual allergen safety. Examples are not an exhaustive inventory.

The complete original guide is low confidence and untested.
