The [complete design](ontario-core4-french-reading-design-2026-11-10.json) binds each official code to its actual French reading tasks: strategies before/during/after reading, meaning from varied supported print, fluent familiar reading and confirmed unfamiliar-word meaning. Original book pages and a 26-word bank make connected reading concrete. Illustrations add compatible details; they do not override literal text. The described original drawings and teacher-checked dictionary entries must be prepared before use.

General strategy mastery or an English reading score does not establish French reading. Every selected operation is checked using actual French print with the learner; teacher support cannot replace it. The existing sight-word and accuracy checks preserve whole topics and are not newly prescribed Ontario norms. Full denotes the complete source-fit design, not observed attainment or tested teaching efficacy.

Decoding and letter knowledge retain their supporting roles; all other edges and complete existing topic assessments remain unchanged. These corrections also have positive witnesses within English and do not depend on creating French duplicates.
