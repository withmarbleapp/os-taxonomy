## A3 independent knowledge explanation and fresh transfer

Remove model answers and completed relationship diagrams. Give the actual source-labelled selections and a blank dated relationship board with three uncompleted interaction-category fields. The learner chooses supported examples from every category, identifies the actual peoples/parties, places and periods, and describes significant features that help explain the relationships. At least one feature must concern each studied Indigenous arrangement and the learned New France institutional arrangement; a list of names or pictures is insufficient.

Require the learner to construct an original connected explanation of who interacted, how, why the interaction mattered, and what the available source establishes. They must compare a supported cooperative relationship with a conflict or unequal-power relationship, without assuming all parties had identical interests, benefits meant consent, or every relationship followed one fixed pattern. Explain a significant consequence using the actual dated evidence; distinguish that account from a conditional further inference and from a modern legal/title conclusion.

For a fresh changed case, supply two explicitly fictional museum captions. The first puts a modern elected role into a seventeenth-century account; the second treats the existence of trade as proof all partners agreed with every colonial action. The learner corrects each using the actual studied relationships and explains which further evidence would be needed for an unsupported claim. Then change a transport or competing-enterprise condition in a declared model and ask which relationship or likely consequence could change and why. No fictional caption or conditional model is reported as a historical event.

Finally require an independent synthesis linking one among-Indigenous relationship, one among-European relationship and one Indigenous–European relationship, with actual periods, significant features, evidence and limits. Assess that synthesis directly against A3's own full wording. It is not an inquiry-process-only answer, a rollup of child labels, a compulsory all-Nations inventory, or a claim every possible group interacted with every other group. The prior complete fixed knowledge tasks remain prerequisites to awarding their respective broader whole topics.
