# Early energy and food-chain factual corrections

This authoring change corrects three existing teaching guides and two food-chain
cards. It adds no topics, curriculum associations, ages or graph relationships,
and does not upgrade an expectation's coverage disposition.

## Electrical-device classification

`mt_9nxyFoYD_b` retains its ten-appliance naming, mains/battery classification,
electrical-function evidence and complete existing assessment. Its guide no longer
treats battery power as permission to open a household device. Classification uses
pictures or intact devices with compartments closed. Any optional circuit activity
is separate and requires an adult-selected, age-appropriate low-voltage educational
kit used with supervision and its instructions.

The targeted safety review read the prevention section within [Health Canada's
button-battery brochure](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/household-products/battery-safety/resources/brochure-button-batteries.html).
Only prevention guidance supports this correction; clinical instructions and
statistics are outside the teaching content. Existing source flags and confidence
are preserved, with one new source and a bounded review note.

## Food-chain producer boundaries

`mt_EygMHKs8Ed` (Simple Food Chains) retains learner-created chains with at least
three organisms, food-to-eater arrows, producer/consumer understanding and reasoning
about losing the producer. Its green-plant starting point now applies to the
studied sunlight-based cases. The claim that every food chain begins with a plant,
or that only plants produce food, is removed from the card and active guidance.

`mt_l6OpmOKMuT` (Ocean Food Chains) likewise retains its chain sequencing,
arrow interpretation and consumer-dependence reasoning. Its description and
producer evidence now specify a selected sunlit marine chain. Animal consumers
in that model depend on their food sources; this is not a claim that producers
must eat other organisms or that every ocean chain begins with a plant.

The targeted review read [NOAA Ocean Exploration's account of photosynthesis and
chemosynthesis](https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/ocean-fact/photochemo/). Its examples of
production without sunlight disprove the former universal. They are teacher
background for bounding the models, not new learner vocabulary or mechanism gates.
Both complete guides and assessments were reviewed, including feedback and full
response rubrics. Existing citations retain their original verification flags;
only the newly read NOAA reference is added as verified.

## Preservation and publication

Stable IDs, all age intervals and scoped curriculum contexts, dependencies,
remaining card fields, unrelated guides and historical confidence/provenance
metadata are preserved. Generation updates only the resulting artifact bindings.
No learner records, estimates or downstream application state are changed.
Deployment and any consumer compatibility review remain separate from this
public authoring correction.
