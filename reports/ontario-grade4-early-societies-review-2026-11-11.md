## Remaining source condition

A3.10 remains Partial, and integrated A3 retains that condition. The current early
Inuit evidence supports cooperative harvesting and a qualified leadership model.
The Greenland National Museum's historical summer-camp account also supports
conflict resolution and alliances, but does not establish that function before
1500. Precolonial mobility, a later revival or an ancient drum fragment cannot
silently supply that date/function relationship.

The resolution needs an accurately attributed early-period account or supported
historical interpretation of the institutional attempt. It does not require a
precisely dated dispute event, a new topic, ceremony or learner records. The other
reviewed tasks can proceed while this source condition remains explicit.
