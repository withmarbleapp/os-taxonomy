# Primary curriculum source integration

This draft combines the current reviewed Ontario and India source proposals into one branch targeting `main`. It is a delivery path for the dependent source stacks, with their commit history and slice PRs retained for review. It does not mark Ontario complete or publish anything downstream.

The [structured report](primary-curriculum-integration-2026-11-11.json) pins ten immutable source leaves: [India #138](https://github.com/withmarbleapp/os-taxonomy/pull/138), [Ontario French #136](https://github.com/withmarbleapp/os-taxonomy/pull/136), [Ontario Social Studies through Grade4 #137](https://github.com/withmarbleapp/os-taxonomy/pull/137), [Grade2 #117](https://github.com/withmarbleapp/os-taxonomy/pull/117) and [archive correction #114](https://github.com/withmarbleapp/os-taxonomy/pull/114). The other leaves are the [completed Grade5 government strand](https://github.com/withmarbleapp/os-taxonomy/tree/ccc57097924ad0458c6f0abfcbf8d1a352960b2a) and [two shared Arts operations](https://github.com/withmarbleapp/os-taxonomy/tree/6211a409df2e27bf31de81318e05b36e0cf732f6). The eighth leaf contains [two citation-only repairs](research-citation-locators-2026-11-11.md) and their validation guard. The ninth leaf makes explicit [new-guide metadata and original summary corrections](research-projection-metadata-2026-11-11.md) required by the read-only publication roundtrip. The tenth leaf applies the existing exporter’s canonical research-record ordering, preserving every complete guide by ID. Their ancestors retain the individual source-fit designs and claim/dependency reviews.

## Combined contents

The current snapshot has 1,904 topics, 4,001 dependencies, 8,313 retained source standards, 177 clusters, 1,089 teaching guides and 4,796 scoped contexts. Compared with the accepted base, it contains 46 added topics, seven explicitly reviewed old-claim revisions, 43 added guides, four reviewed old-guide corrections (two teaching corrections and two citation-only repairs), seven added dependencies, 21 reviewed dependency corrections and one appended cluster. The source catalogue is unchanged. These counts describe the combined proposal; they do not imply that every added claim has a canonical teaching guide or that every source record has Full coverage.

All 570 accepted India positions have a proposed review disposition: 492 Full authoring, 37 mixed skill/contextual, 28 conditional bindings, 12 contextual and one Unverified musical-scale phrase. Ontario French accounts for all 594 primary positions with 570 Full authoring and 24 Partial pending actual local-community resources. Ontario Social Studies Grades 1–4 accounts for 161 positions, 159 Full authoring and two chronology-limited Partial. The Grade5 government strand adds 21 completed source-fit designs through nine reused topics and three bounded Canadian knowledge additions. Its [review](ontario-grade5-government-citizens-review-2026-11-11.md) retains the actual First Nation, Métis and Inuit cases and the independent historical strand. Full describes a source-fit authoring programme with its conditions; it is not observed mastery or proven teaching efficacy.

The Arts checkpoint adds one fixed [staff-notation operation](ontario-arts-staff-literacy-2026-11-11.md) with original score panels and one shared [physical paint-mixing operation](ontario-arts-pigment-mixing-2026-11-11.md). Twelve component pairs across nine exact contexts do not certify whole expectations or the 300-record programme. There are no instrument, recipe or grade clones, new dependencies or changes to earlier authored payloads in these two additions.

## Reconciliation

Both country workstreams independently corrected the same English blending-to-fluency gate. The complete 40+ phoneme inventory is not necessary before a child can accurately understand and reread a book matched to taught correspondences. Integration retains one soft edge with India's already reviewed explanation. The [French reading report](ontario-core4-french-reading-design-2026-11-10.json) records both its original explanation and the selected result. No assertion was weakened to accept an arbitrary edge; both full task counterexamples and the other whole-target gates remain.

The archive-reference safeguard and the manifest topic/subject inventory safeguard are both retained in validation. Combining the branches does not remove either check. Conflicting generated arrays and metadata were reconciled from exact row identities, preserving source intervals and the union of codes, then regenerated from the complete combined data. Research coverage was computed from the actual topic/guide inventory; original guide records were checked separately by ID. The final research array uses the existing exporter’s canonical topic-ID order; original slice sequences remain in their source commits.

## Validation and remaining scope

Generation, dataset validation and generated-artifact checks pass. All 225 tests pass, with zero failed or skipped. Independent comparison checks the complete approved authored-payload union with the documented new-guide metadata/summary correction by ID; the research array follows the existing exporter’s declared order, and scoped context/code union is exact. The archive matches #114 exactly. This proves mechanical integration alongside the slice reviews, not fresh scientific verification of every historical teaching citation.

Ontario Grade5 history (20 records) and Grade6 Social Studies continue separately. A full 2,640-record eligible Ontario audit is also reconciling prior subject evidence; the 694 Arts, Health/PE and Kindergarten records still need whole programme qualification. Actual task/resource duties from prior Language and Science Partial reviews also remain; existing links or a historical review label do not complete that work. The optional 128-record Indigenous-language programme remains excluded by the agreed scope; Indigenous content in other subjects remains included. Uncertain local resources, chronology, language/script/art-form choices and the India musical-scale meaning remain explicit.

Main acceptance, deployment compatibility, old-writer/fleet controls and protected-state-preserving staging publication are separate steps. This source branch makes no DB or learner-state change and authorises no reset, transfer or historical observation reinterpretation.
