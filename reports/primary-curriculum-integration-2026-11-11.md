# Primary curriculum source integration

This draft combines the current reviewed Ontario and India source proposals into one branch targeting `main`. It is a delivery path for the dependent source stacks, with their commit history and slice PRs retained for review. It does not mark Ontario complete or publish anything downstream.

The [structured report](primary-curriculum-integration-2026-11-11.json) pins five immutable source leaves: [India #138](https://github.com/withmarbleapp/os-taxonomy/pull/138), [Ontario French #136](https://github.com/withmarbleapp/os-taxonomy/pull/136), [Ontario Social Studies through Grade4 #137](https://github.com/withmarbleapp/os-taxonomy/pull/137), [Grade2 #117](https://github.com/withmarbleapp/os-taxonomy/pull/117) and [archive correction #114](https://github.com/withmarbleapp/os-taxonomy/pull/114). Their ancestors retain the individual source-fit designs and claim/dependency reviews.

## Combined contents

The current snapshot has 1,899 topics, 4,001 dependencies, 8,313 retained source standards, 177 clusters, 1,084 teaching guides and 4,777 scoped contexts. Compared with the accepted base, it contains 41 added topics, seven explicitly reviewed old-claim revisions, 38 added guides, two reviewed old-guide corrections, seven added dependencies, 21 reviewed dependency corrections and one appended cluster. The source catalogue is unchanged. These counts describe the combined proposal; they do not imply that every added claim has a canonical teaching guide or that every source record has Full coverage.

All 570 accepted India positions have a proposed review disposition: 492 Full authoring, 37 mixed skill/contextual, 28 conditional bindings, 12 contextual and one Unverified musical-scale phrase. Ontario French accounts for all 594 primary positions with 570 Full authoring and 24 Partial pending actual local-community resources. Ontario Social Studies Grades 1–4 accounts for 161 positions, 159 Full authoring and two chronology-limited Partial. Full describes a source-fit authoring programme with its conditions; it is not observed mastery or proven teaching efficacy.

## Reconciliation

Both country workstreams independently corrected the same English blending-to-fluency gate. The complete 40+ phoneme inventory is not necessary before a child can accurately understand and reread a book matched to taught correspondences. Integration retains one soft edge with India's already reviewed explanation. The [French reading report](ontario-core4-french-reading-design-2026-11-10.json) records both its original explanation and the selected result. No assertion was weakened to accept an arbitrary edge; both full task counterexamples and the other whole-target gates remain.

The archive-reference safeguard and the manifest topic/subject inventory safeguard are both retained in validation. Combining the branches does not remove either check. Conflicting generated arrays and metadata were reconciled from exact row identities, preserving source intervals and the union of codes, then regenerated from the complete combined data. Research coverage was computed from the actual topic/guide inventory; original guide records and their source order were checked separately.

## Validation and remaining scope

Generation, dataset validation and generated-artifact checks pass. All 211 tests pass, with zero failed or skipped. Independent comparison checks the complete approved authored-payload union and each source's guide sequence; scoped context/code union is exact. The archive matches #114 exactly. This proves mechanical integration alongside the slice reviews, not fresh scientific verification of every historical teaching citation.

Ontario Grades 5–6 Social Studies continue separately. A full 2,640-record eligible Ontario audit is also reconciling prior subject evidence; 694 Arts, Health/PE and Kindergarten records must not be called reviewed merely because some have links. The optional 128-record Indigenous-language programme remains excluded by the agreed scope; Indigenous content in other subjects remains included. Uncertain local resources, chronology, language/script/art-form choices and the India musical-scale meaning remain explicit.

Main acceptance, deployment compatibility, old-writer/fleet controls and protected-state-preserving staging publication are separate steps. This source branch makes no DB or learner-state change and authorises no reset, transfer or historical observation reinterpretation.
