## Existing-topic decisions

- **Ecology:** existing habitat provisions, food-chain roles, diagram reading,
  animal diets and plant/animal features supply actual knowledge. Species-removal
  predictions combine with existing threat/protection explanations. No separate
  Ontario food-chain, diet or adaptation progression is introduced.
- **Web operations:** interpreting a supplied web already traces shared organisms
  and several chains. A later claim additionally constructs webs from data.
  The operation difference matters; the later construction requirement is not
  imposed on this earlier expectation, and no full claim is lowered.
- **Light/sound:** existing light sources plus biological emission supply the
  object/living distinction. Material tests, medium propagation, reflection,
  vibration, pitch and volume are real contributions. Vocabulary alone does not
  certify every absorption, straight-path or wave explanation. Later ray-diagram
  and pressure-wave claims remain candidate-only.
- **Cross-subject devices:** existing computer components already name cameras,
  microphones, screens and speakers and choose them for a purpose. Combined with
  senses/eye claims, these supply sensory/device cases without a new topic.
- **Environmental effects:** the insect-conservation claim explicitly describes
  artificial-light disruption. It contributes directly, while sound cases and
  relevant perspectives remain conditions. A generic pollution link is not
  substituted for a checked effect.
- **Mechanisms:** the existing lever/pulley/gear claim directly explains force
  changes and everyday uses. It does not automatically prove every part, rotary
  transfer or geometric-plane transformation. Those conditions remain explicit;
  no new mechanism claims are requested in this batch.
- **Rock cycle:** an existing complete claim traces transformations and geological
  time. Its actual assessed operation matches the source. A parallel existing
  record substantially overlaps that operation; it is recorded for later identity
  review, with both IDs unchanged. Age labels do not justify separate claims.
- **Fossils:** formation and interpretation of fossil evidence are different
  contributions. A related strata/landscape inference record is not treated as
  equivalent merely because its title also mentions fossil formation.
- **Community knowledge:** general mineral properties and use examples are
  available, but do not establish geological knowledge held by First Nations, Métis and Inuit
  communities. Checked community sources and knowledge/purpose connections remain
  necessary; Australian cases are not relabelled.
