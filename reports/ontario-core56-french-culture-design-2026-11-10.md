# Ontario Core French Grades 5–6: community culture

This [24-record design](ontario-core56-french-culture-design-2026-11-10.json) accounts for the remaining Core French culture expectations, 12 per grade, through 54 component pairs. It reuses five whole operations and adds two bounded particular-knowledge topics, two complete original low-confidence guides, four Ontario contexts and eight context extensions. There are no new prerequisites or changes to existing authored claims or guides.

The actual [2013 source](https://www.dcp.edu.gov.on.ca/en/curriculum/elementary-fsl) requires different community knowledge: Quebec in Grade 5 and eastern, western and northern Canada in Grade 6. Portable investigation, identity, cultural-diversity and geographic-comparison skills do not establish these particular facts. The JSON documents nine whole-claim comparisons, including the separate political-geography proposal in PR128, which is neither inherited nor required here. No topic is added per institution, city, creator, medium or school grade.

The annual-report page was also visually inspected. Institutional mission, documented activity and demonstrated impact remain distinct. No full film, artwork, exhibition or book contents are inferred; the mural caption is evidence of the attributed collaboration, not a claim to have inspected its image.

Eight protocols retain actual French listening, speaking, reading and writing. Each includes all three regional cases, a non-sensitive actual own/class connection, another community comparison and the earlier France/UK examples for the overall world-community scope. Speaking and writing explicitly explain the regional outward contribution in French: international creative work for Quebec and documented Canadian/Belgian cinema recognition for the eastern case. Comparing with France alone does not establish an outward contribution.

Selected actual institutional French passages are bound for reading, alongside original supported teaching paraphrases. Whole prior picture/text and writing-audience/planning tasks remain required, as do retained familiar French vocabulary and exact written-form checks in the actual corpus. A stronger-language factual answer can isolate the knowledge claim; it cannot stand for the source's French-channel performance. Role changes and stated address preferences reuse the complete existing role-language operation, without assuming all adults require *vous*. Original fictional dialogues are not community testimony.

Both new guides require independent learned place/activity/contribution accounts and a supported comparison with a fresh attributed account. They reject caption copying, generic respect statements and assumptions that every resident shares one ancestry, language or practice. Teaching designs are untested; verified factual sources do not establish teaching efficacy.

Together with the earlier [Core Grade 4 designs](ontario-core4-french-culture-design-2026-11-10.md) and [Grades 5–6 shared-operation designs](ontario-core56-french-shared-design-2026-11-10.md), this proposal completes source-fit designs for all 132 retained primary Core French records. A whole-set guard reconciles exact catalogue identities and every scoped association across the reports. The other 462 Extended/Immersion records remain in scope. Repository acceptance, observed learner attainment and downstream staging publication are separate; this proposal changes no private learner state.
