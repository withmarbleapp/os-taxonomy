# NCERT Arts: first 88 source records

This proposal reviews six formal Foundational records, 30 suggestive Grades 1–2 source positions and 52 formal Preparatory records. It advances the proposed primary readiness ledger from 394 to 482 reviewed records out of 570. The remaining 88 are 56 nongraded, shared-stage Arts syllabus positions and 32 The World Around Us records. This is the selected NCERT national baseline, not coverage of every Indian board.

The formal sources retain their actual stage windows: Foundational 3–8 and Preparatory 8–11, with stage-end attainment rather than an identical demand at every age. The suggestive Grades 1–2 positions use the source-supported primary 6–8 view. Selected primary grades do not replace the full Foundational scope. LS1 resource provision and LS2 baseline implementation are applicability conditions, not taxonomy difficulty levels.

## Four shared claims, with existing claims preserved

Four new shared operations account for genuine gaps after whole-claim search across Arts, English, Mathematics, Science and movement:

- **Matching a sounded pitch** (`mt_zwiJAMjtMU`): actually reproduce a heard reference, compare and adjust. Beat replication and vibration knowledge do not supply this operation. Its new contexts are primary 6–8 and Preparatory 8–11; it is not given a preschool context merely because music appears in a broad heading.
- **Creating and shaping musical sound** (`mt_2mb17lUCPD`): make an audible work, listen and purposefully revise its contrast, sound/silence or ending. A formal repeating-rule task does not establish musical shaping. Repeating and nonperiodic works can both be valid; complexity and taste are not scores.
- **Creating and performing expressive movement sequences** (`mt_hu_OZPduxP`): choose, practise, perform and adapt an own expressive sequence. Locomotion, balance, copying a beat and the complete scripts-and-poetry claim each test different operations.
- **Composing visual artwork** (`mt_2Kn5ianVWN`): make an own visual arrangement and deliberately revise a relationship for an intention. Mark control, geometric composition, functional design and a text requiring both writing and visual features do not cover pure visual organisation.

Each has an original, untested, low-confidence performance guide with skill-specific diagnostics and transfer. Thirteen existing topics are reused. Their authored claims, all old guides and all dependencies remain unchanged. Existing rhythm, mark-making, pattern and theatre claims keep their identities. No country, grade, art-medium or difficulty clones are added.

The unchanged scripts-and-poetry claim is assessed with actual script, poem and improvisation panels, meaningful voice/action, silence and stillness. A single freeze pose or copied line cannot stand for its whole operation. Existing broad prerequisite claims remain explicit and are checked separately where relevant; an artwork is not evidence that every inherited language, group or self-regulation claim has been mastered.

## Conditions that remain actual tasks

The protocols preserve actual flat and spatial making, collage and assemblage where required, texture production by manipulating one material, and genuine own choices. They retain vocal work, actual songs and rhythms, varied arrangements and ensemble coordination. The source-specific partner task checks volume, pitch **and** tempo; an instrumental-only or solo clapping proxy cannot close it. The printed pitch-matching attempt remains an actual attempt rather than a silently upgraded professional success norm.

Musical comparisons use accurate introduced styles and the source’s tempo, rhythm, pitch, expressive feeling, lyric and performance dimensions. The pinned framework’s element table supplies the terminology. A justified expressive interpretation is distinct from a universal emotion code. Dance, visual and dramatic comparisons likewise require actual observed features and supported responses.

Local-cultural examples must be accurately attributed and accessible. Natural observations remain separate from fictional artistic interpretation. Creative variation, genuine manageable challenges, actual resource choices, collaboration and different legitimate responses are retained. No compulsory enjoyment, pleasant mood, fixed grip, expensive instrument, athletic norm or forced personal disclosure is introduced.

The four LS2 positions retain their original identities and complete parameterised tasks. Twelve form-specific candidate contributions are explicitly conditional; only genuinely common collaboration/response components receive flat associations. Selecting music, dance or theatre at a school is an implementation condition, not an instruction to master every alternative, an exclusion from scope or a request for a national art-form choice.
