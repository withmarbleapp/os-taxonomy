## Source and stage meaning

The selected source is the official [NCF-SE 2023 framework](https://ncert.nic.in/pdf/NCFSE-2023-August_2023.pdf), **First Edition October 2024**, ISBN 978-93-5292-741-8. Pinned SHA-256: `0e2c5a1ddb48f758a5e9f867258da40d57f9f236b37fc7f8022261973425f099`. PDF page **214**, printed page **200**, Table **1.1iii**, supplies the selected CG-8 block. Its original layout and every selected row were checked against the verified private preparation artifact; source wording is not redistributed.

The IN interval **3–8** records the official full Foundational Stage, including Balvatika and Grades 1–2. It is a **stage window with a stage-end attainment reference**, not a statement that every linked performance is expected unchanged at every age, nor an invented deadline for an individual grade. The selected primary view is Grades 1–2, but the published formal source has not been clipped into fabricated per-grade standards. The age-only compatibility projection cannot carry that distinction; consumers must retain this qualification when interpreting the association.

Keys use the existing `ncert-ncfse-2023-ed2024` source and generated qualified identifiers. The table below abbreviates the namespace: competency `C-8.1`, for example, means `ncert-ncfse-2023-ed2024:IN-NCERT-NCFSE-2023-ED2024-FOUNDATION-COG-COMP-C-8.1`; the goal uses `...-FOUNDATION-COG-GOAL-CG-8`. These are not newly invented official NCERT codes.

The separate [2025 foundational publication](https://ncert.nic.in/pdf/publication/otherpublications/Learning_Outcome_for_the_Foundational_Stage.pdf) contains contextualisable, suggestive outcome positions. Its 67 Maths positions are **not** silently aliased to these formal records, and remain unreviewed for alignment in this slice.

## Reuse and excluded shortcuts

Every contributing topic was read beyond its title: description, evidence, assessment prompt and prerequisite relationships were checked. Several historical titles were misleading: the skip-counting topic includes multiple step sizes, a similarly named shape-pattern topic is actually about mathematical structure, and one word-problem topic concerns fractions. Those labels did not determine the mapping. Country-neutral cash recognition was found in Life Skills; mathematical notation and money calculations remain separate claims. No rupee-specific duplicate is proposed.

For C-8.14, the search included Mathematics, Learning to Learn and Personal & Social Development. General persistence and Growth Mindset were considered and excluded: their complete claims do not establish a mathematics-specific confidence/value attitude. The Gap does not authorize a new topic or a learner-state change. The taxonomy has no separately tracked difficulty levels; missing performance examples elsewhere in this report must not be turned into numerical-range clones. It first needs a decision about suitable observation and whether that attitude belongs in this mastery taxonomy.
