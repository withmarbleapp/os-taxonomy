# Ontario primary French: final culture review

This [108-record review](ontario-french-programme-culture-design-2026-11-11.json) accounts for every remaining primary Extended/Immersion culture position through 251 component pairs on nine existing topics. It adds seven Ontario contexts and extends 30. There are no new topics, guides, authored claim changes or prerequisite edits.

Of these records, **84 have complete source-fit designs and 24 remain Partial pending actual local-community resources**. Together with the earlier [486 designs](ontario-french-programme-shared-design-2026-11-11.md), all 594 primary French positions now have a reviewed disposition: 570 Full and 24 Partial. No primary French position is omitted or left unreviewed. This is a source-fit ledger, not observed learner attainment or a staging publication receipt.

| Programme scope | Reuse and disposition |

| Extended and Immersion Grades 4–6 | Reuse the exact Ontario, Quebec and eastern/western/northern Canadian knowledge repertoires, actual French inputs and four whole-channel protocols. All 72 records are Full designs. |
| Immersion Grades 1–2 | Actual communities must be local to the learner. The local person/group/place investigation and five shared French/role/picture/audience operations contribute. Eight overall/culture-specific records per grade remain Partial; four finite convention-specific records per grade are Full. |
| Immersion Grade 3 | Requires local communities **and** communities across Ontario. Preserve the whole Ontario repertoire in addition to local investigation. Eight overall/culture-specific records remain Partial; four convention-specific records are Full. |

The source clauses explicitly require local community culture and, in spoken and written accounts, contributions to francophonie and the wider world. A fixed Ottawa/Sudbury example cannot be called local for everyone. The typed `LocalFrenchCommunityBinding` contract therefore requires actual locality, community/group sources and checked bodies, dates/limits, place and group facts, cultural significance, documented outward contribution and prepared French listening/speaking/reading/writing resources. It requires the whole existing local investigation: actual place/building evidence, two supported facts, significance and an individual/group contribution. Its two-account resource set is a design choice, not a requirement for two towns or an official numerical threshold.

The 24 Partial judgments cannot become Full merely because links exist or flags are true. A teaching instance needs the actual binding; no home address, family history or fictional local biography is required. A comparison with France, an aspirational mission or an assumed global audience does not establish the local outward contribution. Completing such an instance does not silently rewrite this public ledger or certify a learner.

The convention-specific tasks are independently concrete: actual French dialogues/messages, leader/helper and host/guest role changes, customer requests versus offers, stated address preferences and fresh writing for different readers. They reuse the complete existing operations, including taught-word retention, meaningful French spellings, whole picture/text and audience/planning tasks where those topics contribute. A single greeting, an English result or copied complete model is insufficient. No universal adult-*vous* rule or separate French social-conventions clone is added.

The 72 later regional records bind the complete earlier [Ontario](ontario-core4-french-culture-design-2026-11-10.md) and [Quebec/east-west-north](ontario-core56-french-culture-design-2026-11-10.md) designs, including the actual institutional passages and verified regional outward-contribution input/output. Overall tasks retain both culture and conventions plus own/other/world-community comparisons. Source support and complexity remain appropriate to the actual programme; no old English or different-corpus result automatically transfers.

All source bodies, embedded examples and nested teaching tips were read. The original English PDF's printed page 156 restores the omitted opening for Extended 6 A3.2; source identity and pinned delivery wording remain unchanged. The complete-set guard checks all 594 catalogue identities, dispositions and scoped pairs, including the 24 local conditions. Source associations remain component contributions; Partial applicability is explicit rather than concealed by a link count. Existing authored claims, guides, edges and exact prior intervals remain intact, and no learner state is changed.
