# Grade 1 own-school locality correction

The actual B3.1 expectation requires identifying the Indigenous and treaty territory or territories of the learner’s own school. The retained mapping provided two verified public-school examples and a careful local preparation protocol, but no target school or completed local source packet. Those examples establish their bounded regional facts; they cannot establish every school’s territory.

The current mapping therefore records B3.1 as Partial for local-resource binding. B3 also remains Partial because its own coherent description uses significant features and services of the same actual community, including the locally attributed school packet in its authored integrated task. This follows review of the overall’s actual community demand, rather than mechanically counting child labels. Grade 1 now has 37 Full and two Partial; Grades 1–4 have 157 Full, two chronology Partials and two local-resource Partials.

The typed `localSchoolBinding` in the [mapping](ontario-grade1-social-studies-mapping-2026-11-10.json) is deliberately unfilled. To complete the application, select the actual school’s publicly available location without collecting a child’s identity or private address, bind dated relevant Nation/community territory and applicable treaty sources, attribute school/board or municipal accounts separately, retain supported overlapping accounts and uncertainties, and connect the verified facts to the same local community map/services task. The two worked cases can support instruction and transfer, but cannot silently fill these fields. Missing or conflicting treaty information must remain explicit; no treaty, exclusivity or legal parcel conclusion is invented.

This correction preserves every dataset file, topic ID, whole claim, guide, prerequisite and scoped association. The earlier source-fit and inquiry protocols remain useful. Ordinary personal responses and direct observations do not require a preselected private child or fabricated completed performance; this correction concerns externally verifiable local territory/community facts explicitly required by the source and the integrated task. It introduces no learner-state write, score change, new topic or Australian-to-Canadian mastery transfer.
