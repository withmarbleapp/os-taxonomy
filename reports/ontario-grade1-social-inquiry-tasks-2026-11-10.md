## A2: actual roles, relationships and sense of self

Use a real, non-sensitive learner/pupil/helper role and an actual starting-school or new-class event. An adult prepares dated classroom/routine accounts, relevant object/place information, an accurate familiar plan and a small factual role/responsibility table. The learner's voluntary own account of capability or self-description is firsthand information. A fictional rehearsal does not replace the authentic application, and the task does not impose a positive feeling or assume every event changes identity.

The learner asks an investigation question, gathers/organizes suitable information and constructs a meaningful familiar-place map within that question. A timeline and role/duty table help interpret continuity/change. A count/display must materially answer the question using actual comparable records. For changing responsibilities, an adult supplies complete dated duty/action records with the same observation unit and period, distinguishing expected assignments from completed actions. A relevant labelled diagram and people/place-care classification key support interpretation; a map legend alone is not that key. No graph of source mentions is forced merely to tick a field. Duty counts do not measure confidence or identity.

Evaluate which dated information supports the question, which is irrelevant and what remains unknown. Conclude by connecting actual circumstances, roles/relationships/duties and the learner's own self-description, including continuity where appropriate. Make a tentative prediction about another transition and suggest how a clearer date, another suitable account or a better question could improve the inquiry. Communicate supported findings with accurate vocabulary and attribution in an appropriate chosen form.

Existing role/duty and qualities/identity knowledge supplies the relationship being investigated. Existing question/source/timeline/map/communication claims supply components. A polished chart or a feeling label without that relationship is a near miss. The source-fit protocol addresses the actual inquiry; it does not infer mastery by adding links.

## B2: people, natural/built features and effects over time

Use an actual own-community feature-change packet with dated earlier/current evidence, an accurate map and clearly labelled data. Distinguish direct observation, documentary statements, project intentions and inferences. The learner investigates people–place interactions with both immediate and longer-term effects, constructs/analyses a meaningful map and interprets more than one suitable representation.

The qualitative Love Park worked case stays qualitative. Whole data-display performance is grounded in a prepared actual own-community packet when the chosen question needs comparable counts: define observation units, site boundaries, times and record coverage, for example visible litter items in the same mapped path/planted-bed survey zones before/after a local change. Use a meaningful location/feature classification key with consistent exclusions. Do not graph source mentions, invent physical counts or force an irrelevant display. Evaluate relevance and limits, draw a supported time-sensitive conclusion, predict tentatively and suggest a better comparison/source or observation. A changed-place transfer checks the inquiry operation rather than copying one conclusion.

The verified worked case is [Waterfront Toronto's Love Park project account](https://www.waterfrontoronto.ca/our-projects/love-park). It documents the former off-ramp removal in 2016–17, construction in 2021–23 and June 2023 opening, retained/new trees, paths and public-space uses. Construction-liaison issues provide a different time scale from the later park. The attributed aerial was privately inspected for visible pond/tree/path/grass features; no image is redistributed or treated as a surveyed north-up map.

This account supports documented physical/project changes, not an independent biodiversity or wellbeing study. No complaint totals, visitor numbers, tree survival or measured health effect is invented. Project intentions remain intentions unless outcome evidence establishes more. The actual learner-community task needs its own verified packet; Love Park does not describe every locality. A synthetic rehearsal dataset must be labelled as such and cannot masquerade as actual community evidence.
