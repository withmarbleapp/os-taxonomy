# Ontario Language: tasks for the 40 retained conditions

This review supplies concrete resource and performance bindings for the 40
previously identified residual positions. It changes no topic, guide, association,
dependency or historical report. All eight dataset files remain byte-identical.

The [cultural-resource dossier](ontario-language-cultural-residual-tasks-2026-11-11.json)
accounts for the 18 specific cultural requirements and their 18 affected overall
contributions across Grades 1–6. It identifies actual First Nation, Métis and Inuit
creator/community texts, three protocols, six grade panels and concrete case checks.
The tasks include actual reading, listening and viewing; Grades 4–6 also enact the
named Indigenous Storywork framework rather than merely recite its principles.

The selected resources include Nipissing First Nation's public language account,
Julie Flett's first-person interview, specified paragraphs of Larry Audlaluk's
publisher-displayed memoir excerpt, Richard Van Camp's poetic opening and the
actual Kenojuak Ashevak museum image. The memoir source is explicitly the
Spring 2020 prepublication catalogue excerpt. No complete book or unseen original
government report is claimed. The ice-accident paragraph is excluded from the
prepared pupil selection. Third-party works and curriculum wording are linked,
not reproduced.

Peer review led to a concrete present-connection task: compare particular
family/place memory and maker relationships with the Nation's public account of
shared language learning. The expected evidence retains differences of voice,
place and purpose. It does not turn one memoir into every contemporary Inuit
person's life or a programme listing into measured programme success.

The [keyboarding task](ontario-language-keyboarding-task-2026-11-11.json)
accounts for the two specific Grade 5–6 performance conditions and their two
affected overall contributions. It requires actual joined-script writing, actual
keyboard use across meaningful texts, honest original/corrected output and a
fresh transfer. Grade 5 word-processing/font choices and Grade 6 accuracy/effect
are explicit. A home-row diagram, finished text alone or handwriting fluency
cannot establish keyboard automaticity. No speed norm, new typing level or
broadened beginner claim is introduced.

Complete authored task binding is distinct from observed pupil attainment.
Actual resource access, learner-relative reading demands, suitable keyboard
access and required performance evidence remain delivery/assessment conditions.
The older childhood-comparison topic retains its own-family interview requirement;
the published memoir does not silently replace it. Other accepted child
contributions and historical Full judgments remain historical: this is a focused
completion of the named residual tasks, not a fresh audit of every Language code.

All 40 official item identities were freshly checked and their normalized core
bodies match the saved source. Every retained key, contributor and internal work
reference resolves. Validation and all 260 tests passed without skips, including
missing-case/resource, absent present connection and fabricated typing-result
regressions. No database or learner-state changes were performed.
