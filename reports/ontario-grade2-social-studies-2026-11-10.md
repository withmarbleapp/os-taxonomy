## Contemporary communities and inquiry conditions

The environmental inquiry and world-community descriptions explicitly require at least one named **contemporary First Nation, Métis or Inuit community**. Generic peoples recognition or a historical Arctic lifestyle card cannot meet that requirement. Attributed current community resources must support location, climate, features, wildlife and ways of life; no claim that climate determines a uniform culture is made. Photographs of clothing alone are not conclusive climate/cultural evidence.

Questioning, gathering, maps and charts are components of coherent inquiry. Evidence relevance, supported conclusions, uncertainty and communication of actual findings remain explicit conditions; making a timeline does not demonstrate evidence evaluation. The machine report distinguishes these conditions from missing knowledge and later whole-claim age fit. Partial counts are not a missing-topic count, and link totals are not a completion percentage.

No learner mastery transfer, DB publication or learner-state action is performed. French programme competence remains separate from English-stream communication; the optional Native Languages exclusion preserves all catalogue records and does not remove mandatory FNMI requirements here.
