# Primary implementation source boundaries

This local audit supplements the selected NCERT framework and applicable CBSE inventory decision. School implementation duties, optional programmes and grade-coded mastery requirements need separate treatment. It adds no catalogue records, associations, topics or coverage gains.

The [current official circular index](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/circulars.html) was checked on 12 November 2026. No updated 2026–27 art-project pairing circular was established by that check. This is a search limitation, not evidence of repeal or permission to carry 2025–26 state pairings forward unchanged. Retain the distinct cover/annex wording of Acad-43/2025 and verify the actual school's period, pairing and project before claiming that implementation binding is complete.

[Acad-12/2026](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/Circulars/2026/12_Circular_2026.pdf), PDF1 clauses1–5, directs schools on menstrual-health infrastructure, materials, disposal, student training and inspections. The circular does not enumerate an I–V mastery table or state a VI+ boundary. Preserve the school-facing compliance and awareness distinction; neither the physical-education exclusion nor the adolescent-health context alone establishes that every related duty is outside the agreed primary profile. Actual age-appropriate curriculum/implementation binding remains to be checked. No clinical advice or newly inferred primary skill is supplied here.

[Acad-26/2026](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/Circulars/2026/26_Circular_2026.pdf), PDF1, identifies the Y-SMilES health programme for ages11–17 and explicitly voluntary participation with parental consent. The overlap at age11 does not make it a universal I–V curricular obligation. Preserve a configured school's optional participation separately; screening and clinical intervention are not taxonomy mastery claims.

[Acad-39/2026](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/Circulars/2026/39_Circular_2026.pdf), PDF1–2, addresses school cyber-awareness activities and resources. Its recommendations, encouragement and parent-facing dissemination are not a newly specified graded attainment table. Preserve the different operative formulations rather than flattening them into a mandatory certificate or social-media account for every primary learner. This implementation source can inform the safety component of the CT/AI material review, but cannot substitute for actual file, app and block-program performance.

These bounded findings leave the historical selected NCERT denominator unchanged and do not establish an overall India or CBSE primary denominator. The next decisions concern actual applicable programme/material bindings and independently assessable requirements; they do not follow from counting circulars or pages.
