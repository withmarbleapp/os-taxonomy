# CBSE constrained listing: one selected primary competency

This bounded proposal adds one association from the CBSE Preparatory C-3 competency to existing topic `mt_gu0NPDvlhY` (Systematic Listing). The [disposition ledger](cbse-constrained-listing-alignment-2026-11-12.json) records the whole-claim contribution, concrete material binding and exclusions. No topic, prerequisite, age, guide or assessment claim changes.

The association is appended to the topic's existing India jurisdiction context, ages 8–11. CBSE's source competency applies once across Classes III–V; the accepted NCF Preparatory stage window supplies the existing local interval. The Grade V material sample does not narrow the standard to Grade V or create identical performance deadlines for Classes III and IV. Topic-global age bounds are not used to invent this context.

The existing claim uses systematic enumeration of two-variable choices. Its accepted [constrained-listing protocol](ncert-preparatory-mathematics-assessment-protocols-2026-11-10.json) already tests the conditions that a simple product-count example could miss: order, repetition, excluded self-pairs, reverse duplicates and a completeness argument. Transfer changes the eligible pool or forbids one pair. The source's constrained enumeration demand can use this claim and protocol without adding a country- or grade-specific topic.

The current [Grade V student companion](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/CurriculumMain27/CTAI_Pri5SH_2026-27.pdf), Chapter 6 question 3, PDF 25 / printed 24, and [teacher companion](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/CurriculumMain27/CTAI_Pri5TH_2026-27.pdf), PDF 43 / printed 42, supply a concrete resource check. The teacher solution explicitly lists the unordered pairings before applying repeated meetings and combining subtotals. Both pages were visually inspected and checked against separate text extraction. These are companion materials, not additional catalogue standards; the contextual game requires no physical PE performance.

Use cards or a two-position table to elicit the list and its explanation. A selected answer or correct arithmetic total alone does not establish systematic enumeration. The learner must explain why no admissible pair is missing, why a self-pair is excluded and when reversing a pair does or does not produce a new choice. Teacher prompts may introduce the task; a supplied list or copied solution is not learner evidence. Supporting arithmetic remains distinct from this enumeration claim.

The selected competency's disposition is **Full for authoring coverage**, not learner attainment. This does not certify any whole goal, graded parent box, handbook chapter or the remaining twenty source positions. C-6's actual computer requirements remain separate. The 521-position selected NCERT coverage denominator is unchanged; source counts do not become skill gaps.
