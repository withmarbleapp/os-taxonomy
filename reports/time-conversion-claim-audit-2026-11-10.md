Other inherited research verification flags do not establish fresh source checks or validated teaching interventions.

The consolidated assessment is broader than any individual historical profile. A retired-ID destination is a requirement disposition, **not automatic learner-mastery equivalence**. Consumers must assess evidence compatibility and decide reassessment or migration separately; no automatic estimate transfer, averaging or reset is authorized. Public acceptance does not publish to any database. The current consumer publisher needs an explicit capability for semantic topic/retirement changes before deployment.
