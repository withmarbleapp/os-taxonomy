# Baloo 2 font source

Baloo 2 is the user-selected typeface for this skill. Unmodified variable font,
weights 400–800. Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/c13390277dfb1fcc7415d84ef7ef9cfc1e52f8c1/ofl/baloo2

`Baloo2-variable.ttf` is renamed from `Baloo2[wght].ttf` without modifying the
font. SHA-256: `d47a6852548059b1db49a1319d06d499d546c3fa2237cf9eee9c43c8abb025c2`.
Its SIL Open Font License and original copyright are in `OFL.txt`. Retain that
notice when embedding or redistributing the font. This asset remains under its
own OFL terms rather than the repository's prose/content license.
